## Four-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** for network and communications security. The book follows synthetic learner **Leo Hart** of the fictional **Quayside Skills Centre**, coached by **Nadia Petrov**, as he helps the owner of the fictional **Bramble Street Bakery** separate customer Wi-Fi, find unknown devices and fix router settings with written permission.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Foundations and first decisions; scope, permission and safe practice | EX001–EX026 |
| 3–4 | Devices, data and dependencies; guided router lab and synthesis | lab_leases.py (3), lab_router.py (4); EX027–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): two unknown devices (bb:77, cc:19) among seven leases, none known but missing; router checklist 2 of 7 pass before changes.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no exploit code, payloads, attack traffic, scanning or bypass steps.
- Changes only with the owner's written agreement, after opening hours, one at a time, with originals recorded.
