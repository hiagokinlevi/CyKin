# Materials — Critical infrastructure and sector security / Rapid course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/critical-sector-security/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons. The English draft covers all 4 chapters, set in one synthetic small town (Larkfield) and written for new learners as a first introduction and first safe practice — not at coordinator or engineering depth.

**Labs and practice:** follow a contained worked example. Each chapter has a synthetic worked example and a paper-based practical activity. Chapter 4 has one short offline Python lab (standard library only) that traces cascading failures across a synthetic dependency map, with its output and two "what if" edits printed in the book, so it can be completed without running code. Use synthetic, sanitized or explicitly authorized data; never touch live systems.

**Projects and assessment:** short quiz (the eight-question course self-check, EX050), observed lab (Lab 4 with prediction-before-output, EX040–EX047) and a small annotated artifact (a one-page service card with a 15-point rubric, EX048–EX049). Every exercise carries a 0–3 rubric.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX050 across the four chapters (13, 13, 12 and 12, matching the planned targets), with worked results in the final appendix of the same book. The draft meets the publication-gate minimum of 50.

**References and glossary:** verifiable primary sources and clear terms belong in the authored manuscript; linked project playbooks retain their source rights.
