## Four-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in information security. The book follows synthetic practice manager **Sofia Lindqvist**, trained by **Mr. Tomasz Nowak** at the fictional **Eastgate Training Centre**, while she secures the fictional **Hollybank Veterinary Practice**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Case and priorities; accounts, second factors, devices and file sharing | lab_sharing (2); EX001–EX026 |
| 3–4 | Phishing, invoice fraud and reporting; backups, incident card, drill and monthly routine | EX027–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 300 synthetic shared files: 47 open to anyone with the link, including marketing 25 of 80, rotas 10 of 20, client 8 of 140 and finance 4 of 60.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
