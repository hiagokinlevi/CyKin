## Four-subject teaching route

This is the one-month **rapid course** for security operations and blue team. The book follows synthetic office manager **Leo Barros** at the fictional **Marlow & Finch** accountancy, supported by the fictional **BrightPath IT**: from a fake invoice sent from partner Elaine Finch's compromised mailbox, through spotting unusual sign-ins and a hidden forwarding rule, to fast containment and ongoing monitoring.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What security operations means for a small firm | EX001–EX013 |
| 2 | Spotting the signs | lab_signins.py (2); EX014–EX026 |
| 3 | Acting fast and calmly | EX027–EX038 |
| 4 | Keeping watch and getting better | EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): e.finch sign-ins Fri 23:47 and Sat 00:02 from BR flagged "new country, night"; all UK daytime sign-ins ok.
