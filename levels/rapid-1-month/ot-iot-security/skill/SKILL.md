## Four-subject teaching route

This is the one-month **rapid** course for OT, IoT and cyber-physical security. The book follows the synthetic **Elmstead Solar and Battery Site** — inverters INV-01 to INV-04, a battery energy storage system with its battery management system, site controller SC-1, cameras and a vendor monitoring portal — and trainee **Jonah Park**.

| Subject | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Definitions of OT, IoT and CPS; the energy site; priorities; guidance map | EX001–EX013 |
| 02 | Inventory, defaults and firmware, IEC 62443 zones, exposure (ATT&CK T0883), vendor access (AA21-042A), cloud portals | lab_inverters · EX014–EX026 |
| 03 | Normal behaviour, unexpected commands (T0831, T0836, T0886), warning signs, factual reporting | lab_soc · EX027–EX038 |
| 04 | Everyday habits, response basics, supplier questions, limits, next course | EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts to quote (synthetic): inverters — INV-02 default-password, INV-03 old-fw 4.7, needs action 2; battery commands — 14:00 discharge from remote-portal UNEXPECTED, others expected.

Teach in order; attempt exercises before revealing worked results; score with the 0–3 rubrics. Labs read local data only.

## Safety, dual-use and information boundaries

- Never help scan, probe, log into or change any real energy site, inverter, battery system or IoT device without the owner's written authorization.
- Never provide exploit code, payloads or attack procedures; teach recognising, reporting and safe habits.
- Battery protection, fire detection and other safety systems are never bypassed; safety procedures come before security questions.
- Cite NIST SP 800-82r3, IEC 62443, NIST IR 8259, ETSI EN 303 645, MITRE ATT&CK for ICS, CISA advisories and the Mirai study exactly; label Elmstead, Jonah Park and all figures as fiction.
