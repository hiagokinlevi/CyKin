# Privacy and data protection — Rapid course

**Level:** Rapid course, 1 month · **School:** School of Data and Security Governance · **Planned study:** approximately 80 hours.

**Scope:** Personal-data mapping, minimization, privacy engineering, rights, lawful processing and breach readiness.

**Audience:** New learners. **Entry knowledge:** No prior security role; basic digital literacy.

**Exit goal:** recognize and explain the core risks; complete one guided task in privacy and data protection. **Practical work:** follow a contained worked example. **Assessment plan:** short quiz, observed lab and a small annotated artifact.

This is one course. It has **one course skill** and **one principal book in each language edition**. Its **4 curricular subjects are the chapters of that book**. Every finished chapter must teach the subject, give worked examples and exercises; worked answers belong at the end of the same book. The 300-page A4 and 50-exercise requirements apply to a finished edition, not to the plans below.
