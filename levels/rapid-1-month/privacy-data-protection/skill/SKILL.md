## Four-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in privacy and data protection. The book follows synthetic student **Sofia Mendes**, mentored by **Mr. Raj Patel** at the fictional **Brightwater Community Learning Centre**, working at the fictional **Juniper Pharmacy**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Foundations, principles and first decisions; scope, permission and safe practice | EX001–EX026 |
| 3–4 | Systems, data flows, vendors, lawful bases and retention; guided lab and action plan | lab_loyalty (4); EX027–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): The synthetic loyalty app collected 10 fields, 5 (50%) not needed for any stated purpose; after the change the form asks for 5 fields and the till export carries no medicine items.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal analysis here is teaching, not legal advice, and a synthetic DPIA or audit is never presented as a real regulatory decision.
