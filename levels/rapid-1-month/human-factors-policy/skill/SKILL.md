## Four-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in human factors and cyber policy. The book follows synthetic learner **Ruth Adeyemi**, guided by **Callum Shaw** at the fictional **Oakmere Adult Learning Centre**, at the fictional **Riverbend Food Bank**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations and three early decisions | EX001–EX013 |
| 2 | Scope, permission, safe report handling and simulation ethics | EX014–EX026 |
| 3 | Systems, data, dependencies and simple fixes | lab_access (3); EX027–EX038 |
| 4 | Guided lab, one-month plan and synthesis | lab_route (4); EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): treasurer reaches 5 of 5 systems; guided lab 7 of 8 correct, median 40 s, 2 over a minute.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they model, count and check, and never probe real systems. Awareness and behaviour claims must cite real sources and name their limits.
- Phishing simulations only with written approval, consent at the organisational level, no punishment of individuals, aggregate reporting and support for staff; never harvest real credentials or personal data.
