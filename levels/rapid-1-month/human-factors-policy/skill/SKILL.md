## English route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in human factors and cyber policy. The book follows synthetic learner **Ruth Adeyemi**, guided by **Callum Shaw** at the fictional **Oakmere Adult Learning Centre**, at the fictional **Riverbend Food Bank**.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they count and check, and never probe real systems. People and organisations are invented.
- Do not invent a volunteer name, a donor name, a supplier name, a phone number, a bank detail, a money amount, or a calendar date. Do not write the shared social password. Do not merge the treasurer with the volunteer who knew that password.
- Twelve staff and sixty volunteers stay separate. 72 is not a census. The genuine forward stays a useful report and is not relabelled as a correct suspicious report. No simulations for now still stands. Ruth does not investigate alone.
