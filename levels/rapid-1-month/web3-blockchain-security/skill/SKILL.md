## Twenty-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in Web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security. The book follows synthetic operations manager **Elena Varga** of the fictional **Copperleaf Coffee Roasters** (four cafés, about 3,000 regular customers), coached by **Mr Samuel Otieno** of the fictional **Quickstart Security Academy**. This Copperleaf is not any other café case. Lessons 1–4 teach the proposal, the written scope, the system map and the loyalty checklist. Lessons 5–20 each settle one later question without replacing the laboratory print.

## Teaching guardrails

- Do not edit `lab_loyalty.py`. Do not print 8/8 or drop a gap line.
- The mint row stays a met 2-of-3 multisig. Do not retune the threshold or name a key holder.
- The 48-hour timelock, the 90-day vendor-access review, and monitoring of role changes and pauses stay gaps. Agreement to fix them is not verification.
- The vendor runs all three nodes. Do not add a fourth node or a Copperleaf-run node.
- Names, emails and purchase details stay off the ledger. The new-address offer is not a completed reissue.
- The monthly balance export and the contractual exit plan stay requests. No compiler version, review date, calendar date, wallet address, amount, staff name or vendor name is printed.
- The pilot stays one café for three months and stays conditional. Four cafés and about 3,000 customers are the business, not the pilot.
- The café's IT contractor verifies. The vendor does not grade itself. The live ledger stays out of scope.
- Do not teach exploit steps, payloads or bypasses. Defender-side synthetic material only.
- After chapter 20 the next stop in this level is course 010. Do not call the scheme secure. Twenty lessons meet the planning minimum and leave review open.
