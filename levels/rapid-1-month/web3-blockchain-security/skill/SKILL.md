## Four-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in Web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security. The book follows synthetic manager **Elena Varga** of the fictional **Copperleaf Coffee Roasters**, coached by **Mr Samuel Otieno** of the fictional **Quickstart Security Academy**, deciding whether to run the café loyalty scheme as points tokens on a vendor's permissioned EVM ledger.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations and decisions | EX001–EX013 |
| 2 | Scope, permission and safe practice | EX014–EX026 |
| 3 | Systems, data and dependencies | EX027–EX038 |
| 4 | Guided lab and applied synthesis | lab_loyalty.py (4); EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): checklist 5/8 passed (62.5%); gaps — no 48 h admin timelock, vendor access not reviewed in 90 days, role changes and pauses unmonitored.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no protocol or vendor is named and no attack steps are given.
- The lab is a read-only, standard-library checklist score.
