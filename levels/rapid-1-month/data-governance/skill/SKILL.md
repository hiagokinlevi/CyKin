## Four-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in data governance. The book follows synthetic student **Kwame Boateng**, mentored by **Ms. Lena Fischer** at the fictional **Riverside Adult Learning Centre**, organising data at the fictional **Copperleaf Café**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Foundations, first decisions, rules, security habits and keeping periods | EX001–EX026 |
| 3–4 | Quality, data locations, suppliers, guided lab and action plan | lab_signups (4); EX027–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 12 synthetic sign-up rows: 2 blank emails, 1 malformed, 2 duplicates; 5 unique valid addresses with a yes tick.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
