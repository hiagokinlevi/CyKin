## Four-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in identity and access management. The book follows synthetic learner **Mia Clarke** of the fictional **Eastgate Skills Centre**, coached by **Joseph Mensah**, as she helps the fictional **Willow Lane Florist** (one shop, six staff) protect its online accounts: email first, personal logins instead of shared passwords, second sign-in steps, former staff removed and a short report for the owner.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations and first decisions | EX001–EX013 |
| 2 | Permission, safe password handling and habits | EX014–EX026 |
| 3 | Listing accounts, users and second steps | lab_accounts.py; EX027–EX038 |
| 4 | Making changes, checklist and report | lab_checklist.py; EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 7 accounts, 3 with a second step, 3 shared (all without a second step); checklist 2/8 before, 7/8 after, recovery details still to do.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no account break-in methods, bypass steps or scanning.
- Never collect staff passwords; the account holder signs in during every change.
