# Principal book plan — Identity and access management / Rapid course / English

**One book for this course and language.** Twenty English chapter-lessons are published. Each numbered chapter is one study day; five consecutive lessons form one suggested study week. The older index had four subject modules. The rapid planning minimum is 20 lessons, and there is no maximum. Duration: 1 month; planned effort: approximately 80 hours. Audience: New learners. Entry knowledge: No prior security role; basic digital literacy. Intended outcome: recognize and explain the core risks; complete one guided task in identity and access management.

**Editorial state:** published English manuscript; technical, language, source, and accessibility review still open. English reference edition. Not a finished qualification. Page count is not the writing target.

## Book requirements

Every chapter needs teaching, definitions, a worked example, practical activity, errors and limits, exercises and a synthesis. There is no page quota and no exercise quota. The final appendix holds answers keyed to the exercise IDs. Cite verifiable sources, label synthetic cases, and review for technical accuracy, rights and accessibility.
