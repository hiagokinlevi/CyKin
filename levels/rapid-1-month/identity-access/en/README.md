This language edition has one principal study book. Twenty English chapter-lessons are published, with worked answers in the final appendix. Each numbered chapter is one lesson; the reader groups five consecutive chapters into a study week without making another copy of the text. The rapid planning minimum is 20 lessons, and there is no maximum. These twenty lessons meet that minimum, but this is not a finished qualification. Review remains open. Other languages remain their own editions.

English is the reference edition. English, Portuguese and Spanish are current lesson-writing priorities; every edition requires its own technical and language review.

**Publication:** technical, source, rights, language and accessibility review still govern a finished edition. Page count is not the writing target. Course titles do not confer accreditation.
