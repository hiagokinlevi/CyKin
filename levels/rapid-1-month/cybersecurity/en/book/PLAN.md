# Principal book plan — Cybersecurity / Rapid course / English

**One book for this course and language.** 4 curricular subjects are its chapters. Duration: 1 month; planned effort: approximately 80 hours. Audience: New learners. Entry knowledge: No prior security role; basic digital literacy. Intended outcome: recognize and explain the core risks; complete one guided task in cybersecurity.

**Editorial state:** draft manuscript exists; release A4 validation and review pending. English reference edition.

## Book requirements

A finished book must contain at least 300 actual A4 pages and 50 substantive exercises with worked results. The current 320-page budget is a plan, not measured pages. Every chapter needs teaching, definitions, a worked example, practical activity, errors and limits, exercises and a synthesis. The final appendix holds answers keyed to the exercise IDs. Cite verifiable sources, label synthetic cases, and review for technical accuracy, rights and accessibility.

## Primary source starting points

- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20) — a taxonomy of security outcomes and a source for discussing organizational risk; it does not prescribe a single implementation.
- [NIST CSF 2.0 FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework/faqs) — explanations of framework components and the Govern function.
- [NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 publication and current control-catalog update](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final) — a flexible security and privacy control catalog. Check the linked newer catalog release before citing specific controls; framework mappings are not automatic equivalences.

These sources guide the English teaching plan. The Harbor Library case is synthetic; factual claims in authored lessons need source-specific citations.

## Practical and assessment plan

Practice: follow a contained worked example. Assessment: short quiz, observed lab and a small annotated artifact. Allocate at least one authored exercise to every chapter and at least 50 in the whole book. Each technical exercise needs a goal, inputs, authorized scope, steps, expected evidence and a worked or rubric-based result. The final book requires a glossary, references and an A4 rendering verified under the [book standard](../../../../../BOOK_STANDARD.md).
