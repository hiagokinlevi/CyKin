## Twenty-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in systems, endpoints and hardware security. The book follows synthetic volunteer **Leah Morgan**, taking a course from the fictional **Northgate Learning**, as she secures the laptops of the fictional **Fenwick Community Food Bank**. Northgate Learning is not Northgate Learning Centre, and Leah Morgan is not Leah Brennan. Lessons 1–4 teach the device list, accounts and settings, encryption and backups, and the monthly routine. Lessons 5–20 teach the mechanisms the quick-check print does not settle. The supplementary container project is not this food bank and is not a control the checklist measured.

## Teaching guardrails

- Do not edit `lab_quickcheck.py`. Do not print a second matrix. The later twelve-of-twelve reading stays prose about the same five questions.
- The dictionary has twelve keys, `FB01` through `FB12`. Column sums are 10, 8, 8, 11 and 9. The conjunction is 3 of 12: `FB01`, `FB08` and `FB12`. Yes-cells are 46 of 60. Failure-set sizes sum to 14 across a union of nine keys. `FB07` has sum 1 and is the unique minimum. There is no tie and no kappa.
- Update zeros are `FB03` and `FB07`. The full-disk cause and the paused-update cause are not assigned to those keys. No day count, product version, vulnerability identifier, or EPSS value is printed.
- Encryption zeros are `FB02`, `FB06`, `FB07` and `FB11`. Standard-user zeros are `FB02`, `FB04`, `FB07` and `FB09`. The only screen-lock zero is `FB05`, whose encryption cell is 1. Backup zeros are `FB03`, `FB07` and `FB10`.
- A serial number names a device. It does not read the disk. No serial string, police report number, or insurer name is printed. The cupboard laptop was wiped and is not an `FB` key. The front-desk computer is not in the denominator. Fourteen email accounts are not listed and are not fourteen laptops.
- The separate administrator secret stays in the password manager, known to Leah and the unnamed deputy manager. No password is written. A standard account is a privilege boundary, not a detector. No detection rate is added. No application-control policy, publisher rule, or hash is in the file.
- Five minutes is a later checklist choice, not a value in the script. A screen lock is not volume encryption and not a hardware patch. No speculative-execution finding and no Secure Boot state are recorded.
- Previous versions are kept for thirty days. The recycle bin has no separate printed lifetime. Two restore tests stay prose. A passed restore does not flip the backup zeros.
- TPM mode avoids an extra startup password by unsealing when measurements match. No platform-configuration value, firmware version, or recovery prompt is recorded. The older laptop whose TPM was off stays unnamed. Do not merge it with `FB07`.
- The recovery key is in the cloud account and on paper in the office safe, not on the laptop, and not printed. The deputy manager is not said to hold the safe. Key copies are not document copies.
- Tamper protection is a Defender setting on a four-line paper checklist. It is not measured boot, not a STIG score, and not a Linux kernel setting. No firewall rule and no network profile are exported.
- Email MFA uses an authenticator and backup codes in the safe. No code, phone number, or sign-in log is printed. Phones are not laptop rows. Phone verification before a reset is not the backup-code path.
- Same-day disablement is an account action. It does not wipe a disk. The cupboard wipe is a separate disposal. No leaver is named. No remote-wipe product is named. A joiner does not receive the administrator account.
- End of servicing is undated and has no Windows version. The management service is not deployed. The fleet is still twelve laptops. `FB07` remains the kind of device in the replacement note.
- Do not teach exploit steps, payloads, wipe procedures, or bypasses. Defender-side synthetic material only.
- After chapter 20 the next stop in this level is course 013. Do not call the food bank secure. Twenty lessons meet the planning minimum and leave review open.
