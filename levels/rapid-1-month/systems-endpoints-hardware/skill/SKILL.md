## Four-week teaching route

This is the one-month **rapid course** for systems, endpoint and hardware security. The book follows synthetic volunteer **Leah Morgan**, taking a course from the fictional **Northgate Learning**, as she secures the twelve laptops of the fictional **Fenwick Community Food Bank** after one was stolen with unknown encryption status.

| Week | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Device list, five-point quick check, updates | lab_quickcheck.py (1); EX001–EX013 |
| 2 | Standard accounts, MFA, key settings, shared PC | EX014–EX026 |
| 3 | BitLocker, recovery keys, cloud backups, restore tests | EX027–EX038 |
| 4 | Re-check, monthly routine, incident first steps, joiners and leavers | EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): updates 10/12, encrypted 8/12, standard user 8/12, screen lock 11/12, backup 9/12; 3 of 12 pass all five; worst FB07. Result after four weeks: 12 of 12 pass all five.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no exploit code, payloads or bypass steps.
- Verify identity before resets; keep recovery keys off the device they protect.
