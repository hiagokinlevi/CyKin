# Subjects and book chapters — Application and software supply chain security / Rapid course / English

**One course, one principal book, one course skill.** Twenty English chapter-lessons are published. The rapid planning minimum is 20 lessons, and there is no maximum. Each numbered chapter is one study day; consecutive sets of five make four pacing weeks in the reader. Links marked Lesson open the published teaching. Review remains open. The lessons are not a finished qualification.

English is the reference edition.
