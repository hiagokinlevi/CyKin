## Four-subject teaching route

This is the one-month **rapid course** for cloud and container security. The book follows synthetic practice manager **Leila Ahmadi** of the fictional **Oakfield Physiotherapy**, taught by **Ben Hartley** at the fictional **Westbrook Community College**, after a patient letter folder was found shared with anyone holding the link.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Foundations and decisions; scope, permission and safe practice | EX001–EX026 |
| 3–4 | Systems, data and dependencies; guided lab and synthesis | lab_sharing.py (3), lab_routine.py (4); EX027–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 3 of 8 storage items (all sensitive) were shared too widely (patient-letters, invoices, old-backup.zip); the monthly routine went 3/6 (May), 5/6 (June), 6/6 (July).

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Synthetic configurations, records and data only; no exploit code, payloads, attack procedures or instructions for bypassing controls.
- Standard-library labs that only read synthetic records.
- Work uses the provider's own reports within the clinic's authority; evidence is preserved and notification decisions rest with the data controller.
