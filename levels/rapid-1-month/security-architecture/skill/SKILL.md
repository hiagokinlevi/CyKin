## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they count and check, and never probe real systems. People and organisations are invented.
- Do not invent a staff name, a member name, a vendor name, a provider name, an approval date, an acceptance date, or a credential. Do not write the break-glass password.
- Six thousand members and two branches stay as introduced. The phone stays on the written list and off the dependency print. The budgeting app stays unsigned. Core banking and the card processor stay outside the written scope.
