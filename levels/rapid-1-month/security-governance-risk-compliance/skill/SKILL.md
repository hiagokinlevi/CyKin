## Twenty-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in security governance, risk and compliance. Chapter 1 is the original author's text through EX005 and is kept unchanged. Later chapters continue the fictional **Northstar Community Services** case. The rapid planning minimum is 20 lessons and there is no maximum. These twenty lessons meet that minimum. The edition remains a working draft. It is not a finished qualification.
