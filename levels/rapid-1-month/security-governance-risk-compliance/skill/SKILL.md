## Four-chapter rapid route

This is the one-month **rapid course** in security governance, risk and compliance. Chapter 1 is the original author's draft and is kept unchanged; Chapters 2–4 continue its fictional **Northstar Community Services** case.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations, strategy and decisions (original draft) | EX001–EX005 |
| 2 | Scope, permission and safe practice | EX006–EX020 |
| 3 | Systems, data and dependencies | lab_deps; EX021–EX035 |
| 4 | Guided lab and applied synthesis | lab_restore; EX036–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): the identity provider supports all three services; the email relay and roster spreadsheet have no owner. The restore exercise totals 245 minutes against a 240-minute target (missed by 5); the database restore is the longest step at 70 minutes, and locating the backup and credentials took 55 minutes.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only and never probe real systems.
- Do not rewrite or re-attribute Chapter 1; treat Northstar details as fictional and figures as illustrations, not benchmarks.
