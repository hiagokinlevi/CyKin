# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Rapid course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [20-lesson index](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Read the book](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** four weekly chapters contain 20 daily lessons in the one principal English book. Each daily lesson includes a worked checkpoint and an exercise. The [chapter index](SUBJECTS.md) assigns the daily order; short plan summaries do not replace authored lessons.

**Labs and practice:** Module 1 (Chapters 01–05) teaches a testable evidence-readiness contract, BSL1, [AR1 authority-readiness checks](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch01-ar1-lab), and a readiness workshop. Module 2 (Chapters 06–10) covers [TRI2 priority reassessment](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch02-tri2-lab), preservation, time, correlation, [HOLD2 source-hold checks](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch02-hold2-lab) and IM2 service-impact activities. Module 3 (Chapters 11–15) includes write-pause validation, [RB3 reversal checks](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch03-rb3-lab), queue reconciliation, isolated recovery and RTE3 measurement. Module 4 (Chapters 16–20) includes tabletop releases, F4-AUDIT, DEL4 delivery/read-back and [BH4 blind handoff](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch04-bh4-lab). These labs use fictional or synthetic data and have worked results in the same book. [Begin Chapter 01](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ch-01).

**Projects and assessment:** Module 4 contains a staged incident tabletop, six-artifact model handoff, independent frozen-packet receiver exercise, two-reviewer L2 calibration, objective-to-evidence measurement contract and model after-action report. The source-reading activity on the British Library's published incident review is separate from the fictional Harbor Library case. The full assessment sequence still needs independent editorial review.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX050 are assigned across all 20 chapters, with 50 matching worked results in the [final appendix](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#answers). Supplemental labs and source reading do not inflate this count. Historical A4 measurements apply to older source hashes; this edition needs a new render and technical, source, rights, answer, language, accessibility and editorial review before complete-book approval.

**References and glossary:** the [manuscript](book/MANUSCRIPT.md) cites primary NIST publications and the British Library's own retrospective report. The cited organizations retain their own rights; supplementary playbooks retain their source rights.
