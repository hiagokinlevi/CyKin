# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Rapid course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons.

**Labs and practice:** Chapter 1 teaches a testable evidence-readiness contract, a booking-behavior baseline with BSL1, [AR1 pre-incident authority-readiness checks](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch01-ar1-lab), and a readiness workshop; Chapter 2 teaches a decision-oriented preservation queue and has contained evidence/time, delayed-arrival, evidence-package, cross-source correlation, [HOLD2 source-hold lifecycle checks](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch02-hold2-lab) and IM2 service-impact activities; Chapter 3 adds write-pause validation, booking reconciliation, a read-only CSV comparison, a backup-candidate replay, MQ3 queue reconciliation and RTE3 recovery measurement; Chapter 4 adds communication replays, the F4-AUDIT release-and-score gate, DEL4 delivery/read-back and [BH4 blind handoff reconstruction](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch04-bh4-lab). These supplementary labs use fictional or synthetic data and have worked results in the book's final appendix. [Read the four chapters](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ch-01).

**Projects and assessment:** Chapter 4 contains a staged incident tabletop, a six-artifact model handoff, an independent receiver exercise with frozen sender and receiver versions, a two-reviewer L2 score-calibration example, an objective-to-evidence measurement contract and an original model after-action report. The book also includes a source-reading activity on the British Library's published incident review, clearly separated from the fictional Harbor Library case. These are draft activities pending independent editorial review.

**Exercises and answers:** 50 numbered exercises are drafted across the four chapters, with 50 matching worked results in the [final appendix](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#answers). The supplementary labs, drills and source-reading activity do not inflate the numbered exercise count. The complete-book gate still requires substantial teaching, technical review and 300 verified A4 pages.

**References and glossary:** the [manuscript](book/MANUSCRIPT.md) cites primary NIST publications and the British Library's own retrospective report. The cited organizations retain their own rights; supplementary playbooks retain their source rights.
