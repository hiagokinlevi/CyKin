# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Rapid course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons.

**Labs and practice:** Chapter 1 teaches a testable evidence-readiness contract, a booking-behavior baseline with the BSL1 lab, and a readiness workshop; Chapter 2 teaches a decision-oriented preservation queue and has two contained evidence/time labs plus delayed-arrival, evidence-package, cross-source correlation and IM2 service-impact measurement activities; Chapter 3 has a write-pause validation, guided booking reconciliation, a read-only CSV comparison, a backup-candidate replay and MQ3 manual-queue reconciliation; Chapter 4 has approval-route and audience-specific communication replays plus the F4-AUDIT release-and-score gate. These sixteen labs or drills use fictional or synthetic data and have worked results in the book's final appendix. [Read the four chapters](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ch-01).

**Projects and assessment:** Chapter 4 contains a staged incident tabletop, a six-artifact model handoff, a two-reviewer L2 score-calibration example, an objective-to-evidence measurement contract and an original model after-action report. The book also includes a source-reading activity on the British Library's published incident review, clearly separated from the fictional Harbor Library case. These are draft activities pending independent editorial review.

**Exercises and answers:** 50 numbered exercises are drafted across the four chapters, with 50 matching worked results in the [final appendix](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#answers). The sixteen supplementary labs or drills and source-reading activity do not inflate the numbered exercise count. The complete-book gate still requires substantial teaching, technical review and 300 verified A4 pages.

**References and glossary:** the [manuscript](book/MANUSCRIPT.md) cites primary NIST publications and the British Library's own retrospective report. The cited organizations retain their own rights; supplementary playbooks retain their source rights.
