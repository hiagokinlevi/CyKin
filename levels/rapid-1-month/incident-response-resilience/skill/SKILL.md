## Chapter 2 priority-reassessment laboratory

Teach [TRI2 reassessment](../en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch02-tri2-teaching) before the other Chapter 2 labs. Have the learner freeze a 09:05 note using only R1–R8, then revise it at 09:20 using R1–R16. Require separate judgments about record availability, task interpretation, cause/actor, potential versus verified harm, source-preservation concern and continuing-activity review. The [TRI2 offline laboratory](../en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch02-tri2-lab) has **20** output lines; ask for predictions before running it, then compare the [worked result](../en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch02-tri2-result). Then have the learner test its three ungrounded-flag negative controls: an R15 retention claim, an R14 quiet-query claim and an R9/R10-dependent B19 interpretation all fail if their supporting cards are absent from the declared release set. In particular, R9 cannot justify the earlier note, R14 is not a universal negative result, R15 has no known expiry anchor, and an approved addendum must explicitly name B19 to narrow that task-scope concern. Its input flags are declared synthetic premises, not authenticated records or an operational priority engine. Use the frozen two-role packet review and deepen EX014, EX016 and EX024 without changing their two-row table, short note, two hypothetical pairs or 50-exercise denominator. TRI2 variants never enter canonical R1–R16.

For the Chapter 2 E1 file lab, use a new empty authorized scratch directory. Its exclusive-create writes must refuse to overwrite either `E1.txt` or `E1-modified.txt`. Ask students to state that its SHA-256 comparison tests only the two local byte strings; it does not authenticate R9 or R14. In the adjacent temporal lab, read `seen=09:20` as learner release, not measured ingestion or operator discovery. For EX018, require the three-row P-A source request and separate two-row P-A/P-B comparison; neither source copy nor hold is completed merely by writing a request.
