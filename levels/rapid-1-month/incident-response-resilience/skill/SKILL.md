## Chapter 2 source-hold confirmation laboratory

Teach [HOLD2](../en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch02-hold2-teaching) after the learner distinguishes a source, its hold control and a collected copy. Keep the case hypothesis **H2** (account use outside the plan) distinct from the **HOLD2** lab label. At the canonical 09:20 checkpoint P-A and P-B remain proposed; R15's 24-hour duration has no confirmed starting anchor or deletion deadline. Ask the learner to predict the 16 baseline lines and three added chronology controls before running the [offline HOLD2 program](../en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch02-hold2-lab), then compare the [worked result](../en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md#ir-rapid-ch02-hold2-result). Require an independent two-role packet review: identify source, objects, event interval, authority, custodian, reported application, bounded non-destructive effect check, oldest-row availability, review term and unresolved limits. Future history, an unavailable custodian, missing oldest row, expired term or release entry never establishes deletion or complete protection. The positive code path checks synthetic assertions only; it does not authenticate authority or a production hold. Deepen EX018, EX019, EX022 and EX024 and require the two-pair escalation artifact to specify 09:35 or an earlier verified trigger without automatic containment. Do not import the invented HOLD2 dates or receipts into R1–R16.
