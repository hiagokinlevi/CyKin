# Incident response and cyber resilience — Rapid course

**Level:** Rapid course, 1 month · **School:** School of Cyber Defense and Operations · **Planned study:** approximately 80 hours.

**Scope:** Incident triage, containment, eradication, crisis management, recovery and continuity.

**Audience:** New learners. **Entry knowledge:** No prior security role; basic digital literacy.

**Exit goal:** recognize and explain the core risks; complete one guided task in incident response and cyber resilience. **Practical work:** follow a contained worked example. **Assessment plan:** short quiz, observed lab and a small annotated artifact.

This is one course. It has **one course skill** and **one principal book in each language edition**. Its **four curricular subjects are modules**. The English book now has **four weekly chapters with 20 authored daily lessons**, one for each weekday in the minimum four-week route. Each daily lesson has teaching, a worked checkpoint, an assigned exercise and a link to its worked result in the book's final appendix. Other language editions remain at their actual published state; an English chapter does not establish a translated lesson. Twenty lessons and one month are baselines, not limits on future depth or additional lessons.
