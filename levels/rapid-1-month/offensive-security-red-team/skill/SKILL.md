## Four-subject teaching route

This is the one-month **rapid** course for authorized offensive security and red team. The book follows the synthetic **Riverside Civic College (RCC)** — staff portal, grading system, guest Wi-Fi — and trainee **Maya Chen**.

| Subject | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Definitions; NIST SP 800-115, PTES, TIBER-EU, CBEST, ATT&CK; what the course refuses | EX001–EX013 |
| 02 | Rules of engagement, scope checking, stop conditions, refusing out-of-scope asks | lab_scope · EX014–EX026 |
| 03 | Findings, evidence, ATT&CK mapping (T1078), severity, audience-aware reporting | EX027–EX038 |
| 04 | Guided synthesis: rank findings, write a one-pager, hold the line under pressure | lab_findings · EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts to quote (synthetic): scope — 192.0.2.5 in scope, 192.0.2.10 EXCLUDED, 192.0.2.130 and 198.51.100.7 OUT OF SCOPE; findings — F1 and F3 score 6 high, F4 score 4 medium, F2 score 2 low.

Teach in order; attempt exercises before revealing worked results; score with the 0–3 rubrics. Labs read local data only; no live scanning.

## Safety, dual-use and information boundaries

- Never help scan, probe, exploit or access any real system without the owner's written authorization and agreed rules of engagement.
- Never provide exploit code, payloads or attack procedures; teach scoping, evidence, ranking and reporting on synthetic data.
- Stop conditions and exclusions (payroll, medical, safety) are never overridden for convenience.
- Cite NIST SP 800-115, PTES, ATT&CK, OWASP WSTG, TIBER-EU and CBEST exactly; label RCC, Maya Chen and all figures as fiction.
