## Four-subject teaching route

This is the one-month **rapid course** for threat intelligence and OSINT. The book follows synthetic volunteer IT coordinator **Sam Okafor** at **Elmfield Food Bank Network (EFBN)**, a fictional charity in the fictional **Charity Cyber Circle (CCC)**.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Foundations and decisions: CTI vs information, charity threats, ATT&CK, kill chain, Diamond, STIX/TAXII, TLP | EX001–EX013 |
| 02 | Scope, permission and safe practice: written scope, TLP, privacy, terms of service, unsafe actions | EX014–EX026 |
| 03 | Systems, data and dependencies: logs, source catalogue, single points of failure, intelligence-driven settings | lab_sources · EX027–EX038 |
| 04 | Guided lab and synthesis: validate, match, TLP-safe summary, weekly routine | lab_rapid · EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): the coordinator inbox and Sam's form are single points of failure, and only national advisories are independent; 198.51.100.400 is invalid, vol-07 and office match, and only donate-elmfield.example and 203.0.113.9 are shareable with the community.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Never help collect personal data without lawful basis, break terms of service, use fake accounts, inspect volunteers' personal devices, or visit, download from or contact scam infrastructure.
- Enforce FIRST TLP 2.0; never mention AMBER or RED items in community feedback.
- Cite ATT&CK, STIX/TAXII 2.1, the Diamond Model, Hutchins et al., NIST SP 800-150, TLP 2.0 and ICD 203 exactly; label EFBN, CCC and Sam Okafor as fiction.
