## Eight-chapter doctoral route

This is the two-year **doctoral research track** in information security. The book follows synthetic candidate **Yusuf Al-Rashid**, supervised by **Prof. Ingeborg Hald** at the fictional **Marlow University**, working with the fictional **Fernvale Municipal Services**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Integrity, methods and the research frontier | lab_category (2); EX001–EX014 |
| 3–4 | Proposal and measurement pilot | lab_privilege (4); EX015–EX026 |
| 5–8 | Main study, validation, critique, archive and defense | lab_patch (5); EX027–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): Synthetic categorisation 3 high and 2 moderate systems; 152 of 600 accounts (25.3%) with excess privilege, 166 excess grants, 34 admin_console without need; median patch latency 14.6 to 10.1 days (adopters) and 12.6 to 11.6 days (comparison), difference-in-differences -3.4 days, 95% bootstrap CI -7.4 to 1.2 (inconclusive).

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
