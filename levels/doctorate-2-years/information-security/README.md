# Information security — Doctoral research track

**Level:** Doctoral research track, 2 years · **School:** School of Cybersecurity Foundations · **Planned study:** approximately 1600 hours.

**Scope:** Protect information confidentiality, integrity and availability through policy, classification, controls and assurance.

**Audience:** Advanced researchers. **Entry knowledge:** Research experience and a defensible original question.

**Exit goal:** test an original contribution and its limits independently in information security. **Practical work:** replicate baselines, challenge assumptions and publish evidence. **Assessment plan:** original research portfolio, thesis and defense plan.

This is one course. It has **one course skill** and **one principal book in each language edition**. Its **8 curricular subjects are the chapters of that book**. Every finished chapter must teach the subject, give worked examples and exercises; worked answers belong at the end of the same book. The 300-page A4 and 50-exercise requirements apply to a finished edition, not to the plans below.
