## Eight-subject teaching route

This is the two-year **doctoral research track** for threat intelligence and OSINT. The book follows synthetic candidate **Tomas Ekberg** at **Larkspur CTI Lab**, whose thesis measures how well estimative confidence language in CTI reports matches later confirmation.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 01–03 | Research integrity (GDPR, ToS, TLP 2.0), frontier and gap, falsifiable proposal | EX001–EX021 |
| 04–05 | Baseline replication and pilot, blind double-coding and independent validation | lab_overlap, lab_calibration, lab_kappa · EX022–EX035 |
| 06–08 | ICD 203 critique, reproducibility archive, defence and publication | EX036–EX056 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): feed overlap Jaccard 0.500; pilot Brier 0.183 with the 0.9 bin observed 0.67; coder kappa 0.710.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Never help collect personal data without a lawful basis, evade platform terms of service, deanonymise people or access adversary infrastructure.
- Respect FIRST TLP 2.0 labels and licences; restricted material appears only as aggregates or hash manifests.
- Cite ATT&CK, STIX/TAXII 2.1, the Diamond Model, Hutchins et al., NIST SP 800-150, TLP 2.0 and ICD 203 exactly; label Larkspur, Tomas Ekberg and Dr. Hana Sato as fiction.
