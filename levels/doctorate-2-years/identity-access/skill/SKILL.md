## Eight-chapter doctoral route

This is the two-year **doctoral research track** for identity and access management. The book follows synthetic candidate **Rhea Mwangi** of the fictional **Thornfield University**, supervised by **Prof. Daniel Osei**, as she studies whether role-model complexity predicts excess privilege and whether a structured access review removes more of it, with three partners led by the fictional **Calder Valley Health Trust**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Integrity and coder agreement; research frontier; registered proposal | lab_kappa.py (1); EX001–EX020 |
| 4–6 | Baseline and pilot; randomised main study with blinded validation; peer critique | lab_shadow.py (4), lab_trial.py (5); EX021–EX038 |
| 7–8 | Synthesis and reproducibility archive; defense and publication | lab_corr.py (7); EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): kappa 0.85 (36/40 agree); 5 of 14 entitlements unused (0.36); removal 8.2% vs 24.8%; Spearman rho 0.90.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no attack procedures, credential attacks or control-bypass steps.
- Partner data only under written agreement, pseudonymised, encrypted and deleted on schedule.
