## Eight-chapter doctoral route

This is the two-year **doctoral research track** in data governance. The book follows synthetic candidate **Nadia Rahman**, supervised by **Prof. Lars Eriksen** at the fictional **Halvorsen University**, working with the fictional **Westmark Statistics Office**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Integrity, methods and the research frontier | lab_quality (2); EX001–EX014 |
| 3–4 | Proposal and replicated linkage baseline | lab_linkage (4); EX015–EX026 |
| 5–8 | Main study, validation, critique, archive and defense | lab_fair (7); EX027–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): Synthetic register completeness 0.798, validity 0.937, uniqueness 0.966; linkage precision/recall 0.776/0.980 at threshold 0, 0.953/0.920 at 4, 0.991/0.838 at 8; FAIR checklist scores 8, 4, 4 and 1 of 8 (mean 0.53).

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
