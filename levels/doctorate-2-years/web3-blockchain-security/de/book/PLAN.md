# Principal book plan — Web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security / Doctoral research track / Deutsch

**One book for this course and language.** 8 curricular subjects are its chapters. Duration: 2 years; planned effort: approximately 1600 hours. Audience: Advanced researchers. Entry knowledge: Research experience and a defensible original question. Intended outcome: test an original contribution and its limits independently in web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security.

**Editorial state:** structured outline; manuscript not yet authored. English working summaries await translation and review.

## Book requirements

A finished book must contain at least 300 actual A4 pages and 50 substantive exercises with worked results. The current 600-page budget is a plan, not measured pages. Every chapter needs teaching, definitions, a worked example, practical activity, errors and limits, exercises and a synthesis. The final appendix holds answers keyed to the exercise IDs. Cite verifiable sources, label synthetic cases, and review for technical accuracy, rights and accessibility.

## Practical and assessment plan

Practice: replicate baselines, challenge assumptions and publish evidence. Assessment: original research portfolio, thesis and defense plan. Allocate at least one authored exercise to every chapter and at least 50 in the whole book. Each technical exercise needs a goal, inputs, authorized scope, steps, expected evidence and a worked or rubric-based result. The final book requires a glossary, references and an A4 rendering verified under the [book standard](../../../../../BOOK_STANDARD.md).
