## Eight-chapter doctoral route

This is the two-year **doctoral research track** in Web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security. The book follows synthetic candidate **Lucía Ferreyra** of the fictional **Kestrel Institute of Technology**, supervised by **Prof. Henrik Aalto**, as she tests whether structured checklist review, mapped to EEA EthTrust requirements and SWC categories, leaves fewer high-severity issues than unstructured review at the fictional **Harbourline Credit Union Consortium**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Research integrity, coder agreement, research frontier, tool and manual overlap | lab_kappa.py (1), lab_overlap.py (2); EX001–EX014 |
| 3–5 | Registered proposal, baseline and pilot, randomised main study | lab_baseline.py (4), lab_trial.py (5); EX015–EX032 |
| 6–8 | Peer critique, synthesis and archive, defense and publication | EX033–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): kappa 0.833 (35/40 agreed); tool/manual Jaccard 0.33 (4 of 12); baseline 17/60 high (28.3%), access control 9; residual high 0.42 vs 1.25, permutation p = 0.0146 (seed 2026), zero-residual 8/12 vs 2/12.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic contracts and findings only; no exploit code, attack steps or payloads.
- Vulnerability data is shared only after fixes, pseudonymised, and published without attack-enabling detail.
