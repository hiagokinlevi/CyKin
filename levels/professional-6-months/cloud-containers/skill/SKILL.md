## Twelve-subject teaching route

This is the six-month **professional course** for cloud and container security. The book follows synthetic practitioner **Hannah Kowalski** of the fictional **Elmstead Council**, supervised by **Dev Patel** and studying at the fictional **Kingsbridge Professional Institute**, after an internal audit found broad cloud roles and unprioritised image vulnerabilities in a parking-permit service.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Foundations, service and stakeholders, authority and evidence, core methods | EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Tools and interpretation, workflow, controls and verification, case analysis | lab_roles.py (5), lab_triage.py (6), lab_confirm.py (7); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Communication and ethics, quality, supervised laboratory, portfolio | EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 6 of 13 granted permissions unused (46%), including iam.admin on reporting and deploy-bot; tier 1 is CVE-A in internet-facing permits-web (severity 9.8, likelihood 0.62); 5 of 6 remediation items confirmed, reporting rebuild open with compensating controls.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Synthetic configurations, records and data only; no exploit code, payloads, attack procedures or instructions for bypassing controls.
- Standard-library labs that only read synthetic records; CVE labels are placeholders.
- Work stays within written authority; findings describe systems and processes, not individuals.
