## Twelve-chapter professional route

This is the six-month **professional course** in security governance, risk and compliance. The book follows synthetic professional **Lena Hoffmann**, mentored by **Mr. Victor Reyes** at the fictional **Ashby Professional Academy**, building GRC practice at the fictional **Marston Credit Union**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Foundations, stakeholders, authorised practice and core risk methods | EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Self-assessment, findings workflow, control testing and a supplier case | lab_csa (5), lab_n (7); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Reporting and ethics, process quality, supervised assurance review and portfolio | lab_age (10); EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): all four domains self-rate above review, vendor management gap +1.33; zero-deviation samples 29 / 59 / 99 for 10% / 5% / 3%; 4 of 8 open findings overdue.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Risk figures are illustrations, not benchmarks.
- Cite standards by exact edition and clause topic; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. A synthetic audit, assessment or SOC 2 report is never presented as a real attestation, and compliance mappings are teaching aids, not legal advice.
