## Twelve-chapter professional route

This is the six-month **professional course** for network and communications security. The book follows synthetic learner **Grace Whitfield** of the fictional **Northgate Professional Academy**, mentored by **Sam Adeyemi**, as she reviews the fictional law firm **Oakline Legal**: guest isolation, remote access with multi-factor authentication, firewall change review, alert triage, a supervised phishing investigation and clear reporting.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Practitioner foundations, service and stakeholder context, authorised evidence handling, core review methods | EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Alert interpretation, operational workflow, change review and verification, supervised case analysis | lab_triage.py (5), lab_review.py (7); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Communication and ethics, quality and troubleshooting, supervised baseline lab, portfolio and final review | lab_baseline.py (11); EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): top triage score 9 (admin login from a new country on case files), then 6 (mass forwarding rule); 2 of 5 change requests approvable as submitted (CR1, CR4); baseline mean 406.9, sd 14.7, threshold 436.2, weeks 10 (512) and 13 (470) above it.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no exploit code, payloads, attack traffic, scanning or bypass steps.
- Written authorisation, need-to-know evidence handling and escalation criteria apply to every task.
