# Materials — Critical infrastructure and sector security / Professional course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/critical-sector-security/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons. The English draft covers all 12 chapters, set in one synthetic water utility (Kestrel Water) and written for coordinators, not technicians.

**Labs and practice:** five short offline Python labs (standard library only) that turn coordination checklists into repeatable checks — consequence-led risk-register ranking and hygiene (Chapter 4), RACI structure and load (Chapter 6), third-party remote-access review (Chapter 8), notification clocks (Chapter 9) and tabletop decision-log quality (Chapter 10). Every output is printed in the book, so the exercises can be done without running code. Chapter 11 is a supervised tabletop-exercise design (exercise "DRY CREEK") with a design rubric. Use synthetic, sanitized or explicitly authorized data; never touch live operational systems.

**Projects and assessment:** portfolio of cases (supplier-access case in Chapter 8, tabletop after-action report in Chapter 11), lab and exercise-design rubrics, and a final practical review with a board briefing and 90-day plan (Chapter 12). Every exercise carries a 0–3 rubric.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX060 across the twelve chapters (five per chapter), with worked results in the final appendix of the same book. The draft meets the publication-gate minimum of 50 and the planned target of five per chapter.

**References and glossary:** verifiable primary sources and clear terms belong in the authored manuscript; linked project playbooks retain their source rights.
