## Twelve-chapter professional route

This is the six-month **professional programme** in Web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security. The book follows synthetic practitioner **Marcus Delgado** of the fictional **Bayview Charity Network**, supervised by **Ms Ingrid Holm** of the fictional **Kestrel Professional Academy**, safeguarding cryptoasset donations held in a 2-of-3 multi-signature treasury and converted monthly through a regulated exchange.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Foundations, service and stakeholders, authorised practice, custody review | EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Reconciliation tools, conversion workflow, controls and verification, supervised case | lab_donations.py (5); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Reporting and ethics, quality and troubleshooting, supervised laboratory, portfolio | lab_treasury.py (11); EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): donations — R-203 75 vs ledger 57, R-205 no reference, tx-a6 40 with no receipt; treasury — departed former-cfo on signer list, P-31 1/2, P-32 2/3 (large), P-33 not allow-listed and 1/2; none ready.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no protocol, exchange or charity is named and no attack steps are given.
- Labs are read-only, standard-library checks; none sign or send transactions.
