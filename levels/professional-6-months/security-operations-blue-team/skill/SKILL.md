## Twelve-subject teaching route

This is the six-month **professional course** for security operations and blue team. The book follows synthetic IT manager **Priya Nair** and analyst **Tom Reyes** at the fictional **Fenwick Engineering**: from a missed night-time compromise of technician account j.harper (new-country VPN login, Domain Admins change, 2,310 design files read), through charter, logging, detection, a managed provider, response planning, investigation, board reporting and maturity, to a first-year review.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Mission and models, governance, log coverage, ATT&CK and Sigma | lab_coverage.py (3); EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Triage pipeline, managed provider, tuning, response planning | lab_precision.py (7); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Investigation, board reporting, maturity, capstone review | lab_timeline.py (9); EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): coverage 6/10 = 60% (critical 4/5, ERP missing); precision 0.33 admin change and AV on server, 0.07 new-country VPN, 0.01 macro; timeline 22:41 VPN, 22:43 Domain Admins add, 22:47 2,310 files read, 22:55 session closed, 22:58 removal.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Standard-library labs; reserved example addresses.
- Staff monitoring for security only, reviewed by HR and the staff council; provider tests are announced and authorised.
