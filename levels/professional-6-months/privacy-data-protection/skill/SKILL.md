## Twelve-chapter professional route

This is the six-month **professional course** in privacy and data protection. The book follows synthetic student **Marta Silva**, mentored by **Ms. Grace Lin** at the fictional **Northgate Professional Academy**, on placement at the fictional **Halden Recruitment**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Security foundations, stakeholders and roles, authorised practice and core methods | lab_cvs (4); EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Tools and interpretation, access request workflow, controls and verification, and a breach case | lab_deadlines (6); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Communication and ethics, quality and troubleshooting, a supervised redaction lab and the final portfolio | lab_redact (11); EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 1,792 of 2,500 candidates (71.7%) were past six months without talent-pool consent, and 216 pool entries had no contact for over two years; 7 of 36 access requests (19.4%) were answered after one month, median 21 days; a six-line response had 4 redactions and 3 third-party names still visible.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal analysis here is teaching, not legal advice, and a synthetic DPIA or audit is never presented as a real regulatory decision.
