## Twelve-subject teaching route

This is the six-month **professional** course for authorized offensive security and red team. The book follows the synthetic **Harborline Credit Union (HCU)** — online banking, staff VPN, AS/400 core out of scope — and junior practitioner **Leo Okonkwo**.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 01–03 | Foundations, CIA, frameworks (NIST SP 800-115, PTES, WSTG, ATT&CK, TIBER-EU, CBEST); services and stakeholders; ROE checklist and evidence | lab_roe · EX001–EX015 |
| 04–07 | PTES methods, tool filtering, day-of workflow, controls and retest verification | lab_severity · EX016–EX035 |
| 08–12 | Case analysis (T1078), ethics and reporting, ticket quality, supervised lab, portfolio | lab_ticket · EX036–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): ROE — MISSING emergency_contact until filled; severity — keep 3 of 5 (drop dir-listing, trace-method); tickets — T-12/T-21 OK, closed-ready 2.

Teach in order; labs read local data only; no live scanning or exploit code.

## Safety, dual-use and information boundaries

- Never help test real systems without written authorization naming scope, exclusions, methods, window and emergency contact.
- Never provide exploit code or attack procedures; teach scoping, validation, reporting and verification on synthetic data.
- Core banking, third parties and personal accounts stay out of scope unless separately authorized.
- Cite NIST SP 800-115, PTES, ATT&CK, OWASP WSTG, TIBER-EU and CBEST exactly; label HCU and Leo Okonkwo as fiction.
