# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Professional course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons.

**Teaching and case:** the [English manuscript](book/MANUSCRIPT.md) contains Chapters 1–5 as a continuous fictional Cedar Training Network case. The learner makes a readiness map, action charter, timed triage, bounded preservation package and three-hypothesis handoff. Cards are released by checkpoint; later evidence cannot be used to justify earlier decisions.

**Labs and practice:** a local 34-byte SHA-256 fixture in Chapter 4 has an exact reproducible result; it is explicitly separate from the case's incomplete IdP export. A supervised role meeting, timed triage board, peer evidence review and hypothesis board require no real tenant access.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX025 provide five task artifacts per authored subject and [worked results in the same book](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#worked-answers). The planned complete course requires 12 chapters and 60 exercises, so seven subjects and 35 exercises remain unwritten; the current draft does not meet the complete-book standard.

**Sources and glossary:** NIST primary publications are cited for general guidance; the CTN case and its decision rubrics are labeled fictional/original. Linked playbooks retain their own source rights.
