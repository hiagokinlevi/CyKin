# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Professional course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons.

**Teaching and case:** the [English manuscript](book/MANUSCRIPT.md) drafts all 12 subjects as a continuous fictional Cedar Training Network case. It progresses from readiness, authority, triage, preservation and investigation through containment, eradication, recovery, communication, playbook quality, supervised simulation and a source-traceable portfolio. Cards C01–C33 are released by checkpoint; later evidence cannot justify earlier decisions.

**Labs and practice:** Chapters 1–3 introduce the offline C1 source-status, C2 action-state and C3 clock-correction fixtures. Chapter 4's C4 byte-integrity variant has three exact lines and is separate from the incomplete IdP export; Chapter 5's C5 join audit has three and cannot infer causation. Chapter 6's C6 state/route fixture has five lines, including a separately labeled V6 failure; Chapter 7's C7 inventory-delta fixture has two; Chapter 8's C8 recovery-gate fixture has four and treats C27's scheduled-route status as unknown. Chapter 9's C9 draft-release fixture has two blocked messages; Chapter 10's C10 playbook-branch fixture has four recommendation-state lines. Each has a worked result in the final appendix. Chapters 11–12 add the five-line L11 release audit and four-line P12 improvement-ticket audit. All fixtures and V-series variants use synthetic data, never a real tenant or main-case observation.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX060 provide five task artifacts per drafted subject and [60 worked results in the same book](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#worked-answers). All chapter positions have a draft start, but the book remains below the 300-page A4 floor and needs substantial expansion and independent review.

**Sources and glossary:** NIST primary publications are cited for general guidance; the CTN case and its decision rubrics are labeled fictional/original. Linked playbooks retain their own source rights.
