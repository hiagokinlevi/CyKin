## Twelve-chapter professional route

This is the six-month **professional course** in data governance. The book follows synthetic participant **Grace Mwangi**, taught by **Mr. Tomasz Nowak** at the fictional **Kingsford Professional Academy**, leading governance at the fictional **Northbank Food Bank Network**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Role, value of governance, roles and a data inventory | EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Policy, data quality, consent and security | lab_emails (6), lab_consent (7); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Suppliers and volunteers, retention and rights, incidents and trustee reporting, action plan | EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 1,684 donor email records: 1,684 distinct as written, 1,500 after normalising, 184 duplicates; consent for 4,000 donors: dated yes 2,507 (62.7%), undated yes 595 (14.9%), no 615 (15.4%), blank 283 (7.1%).

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
