## Twelve-chapter professional route

This is the six-month **professional course** in information security. The book follows synthetic learner **Daniel Reyes**, tutored by **Ms. Helen Ashby** at the fictional **Kingsmere Professional Institute**, while he takes on security at the fictional **Corbridge Accountancy Partners**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | Case, the firm, risk, accounts and MFA, devices and data, payment fraud | lab_risk (3), lab_mfa (4); EX001–EX030 |
| 7–12 | Suppliers, people, incidents, continuity, policy and obligations, reporting to leadership | EX031–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 8 synthetic risks: phishing takeover 16 and invoice fraud 15 rank high, four medium, two low; 70 synthetic accounts: 44 (62.9%) with MFA, staff 39 of 52, partners 4 of 8, administrators 1 of 4, service accounts 0 of 6.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
