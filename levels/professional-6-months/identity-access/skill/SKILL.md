## Twelve-chapter professional route

This is the six-month **professional course** in identity and access management. The book follows synthetic learner **Daniel Okafor** of the fictional **Pinewood Professional Academy**, mentored by **Helen Brooks**, as he reviews identity and access at the fictional **Ashby and Lane Accountants** (three offices, about 60 staff): authentication, role baselines and drift, alert triage, change workflow, guest expiry, a supervised investigation and a final portfolio.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Foundations, standards, authorised evidence, authentication review | EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Role baselines, role drift lab, alert triage, request and change workflow | lab_drift.py (6); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Guest access, supervised investigation, reporting and troubleshooting, final portfolio | lab_guests.py (9); EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 3 of 6 people beyond baseline (k.ng billing, m.cole client-files, t.bell payroll-admin); guests: client-a no expiry and 270 days inactive, client-b expired but enabled, consult no sponsor and no expiry, of 5 checked.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no credential attacks, bypass techniques or scanning.
- Investigations are approved, narrow and factual; labs are read-only reports.
