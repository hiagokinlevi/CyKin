## Twelve-subject teaching route

This is the six-month **professional course** for threat intelligence and OSINT. The book follows synthetic IT support lead **Priya Nair** as she takes on part-time CTI duties at **Bramwell Insurance Brokers (BIB)**, a fictional firm in the fictional **Financial Services Sharing Group (FSSG)**.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 01–04 | Foundations, stakeholders and requirements, authorised practice and evidence, core methods | EX001–EX020 |
| 05–08 | Tools and triage scoring, weekly workflow, controls and verification, supervised fraud case | lab_triage · EX021–EX040 |
| 09–12 | Communication and ethics, quality and troubleshooting, supervised laboratory, portfolio | lab_brief · EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): triage ranks Q3 8 and Q1 7 to analyse, Q4 5 to check, and Q5 and Q2 (4 each) to log only; the brief check passes label, bottom line, confidence and actions but fails "no staff email", so release is held.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Never help collect personal data without lawful basis, break terms of service, use fake accounts, visit criminal forums, or contact adversary infrastructure.
- Enforce FIRST TLP 2.0; keep staff and client details out of products; route regulator, police and takedown actions through management.
- Cite ATT&CK, STIX/TAXII 2.1, the Diamond Model, Hutchins et al., NIST SP 800-150, TLP 2.0 and ICD 203 exactly; label BIB, FSSG and Priya Nair as fiction.
