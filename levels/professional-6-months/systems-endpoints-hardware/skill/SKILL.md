## Twelve-subject teaching route

This is the six-month **professional course** for systems, endpoint and hardware security. The book follows synthetic IT manager **Seun Bakare**, mentored by **Claire Dunmore** on the fictional **Kingsmere Institute** programme, as he closes an audit finding about endpoint hardening at the fictional **Marlowe Freight**.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Charter, hardening standard, coverage by device group, risk-ranked gaps | lab_coverage.py (3), lab_rank.py (4); EX001–EX020 |
| 5–9 | Privileged server access, warehouse PCs, Linux baseline, patching, exceptions | EX021–EX045 |
| 10–12 | Evidence pack, results and sustainment, lessons | EX046–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): weakest coverage cell warehouse PCs encryption 30%; Linux servers 50% baseline, 78% EDR; top-ranked gap server admin without MFA (score 20), then warehouse PCs without EDR (16, effort 1). Programme results: laptop compliance 70→91%, Linux 50→89%, laptop patching 84→95%, admin MFA 0→100%.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no exploit code, payloads or bypass steps.
- Tailoring and exceptions are recorded with reasons, compensating controls, owners and expiry.
