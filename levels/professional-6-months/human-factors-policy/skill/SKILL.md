## Twelve-chapter professional route

This is the six-month **professional course** in human factors and cyber policy. The book follows synthetic practitioner **Grace Mwangi**, mentored by **Alan Pryce** at the fictional **Harlow Professional Academy**, as security coordinator at the fictional **Ferngate Housing Association**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Foundations, service and stakeholder context, authorised practice and evidence handling | EX001–EX015 |
| 4–6 | Core methods, tools and interpretation, operational workflow | lab_barriers (4), lab_verdict (5); EX016–EX030 |
| 7–9 | Controls and verification, supervised case analysis, communication and ethics | lab_nearmiss (8); EX031–EX045 |
| 10–12 | Quality and troubleshooting, supervised laboratory, portfolio and review | lab_officers (10), lab_versions (11); EX046–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): top barrier 51/126 = 40%; median verdict 26 h to 3 h; urgency in 4 of 4 near misses; housing officer reports 16 to 2 to 15; verdict version B 25/30 vs A 18/30.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they model, count and check, and never probe real systems. Awareness and behaviour claims must cite real sources and name their limits.
- Phishing simulations only with written approval, consent at the organisational level, no punishment of individuals, aggregate reporting and support for staff; never harvest real credentials or personal data.
