# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Technologist course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons. The English draft covers all 30 chapters and follows the fictional municipal provider Fernmoor City Services as the learner builds, operates, measures and improves its incident response service.

**Labs and practice:** implement an end-to-end service with monitored controls. Five offline Python labs (standard library only) with expected outputs printed in the book: triage latency with missing records (9), bounded timeline with zone and skew corrections (15), SHA-256 evidence manifest and verification (22), stage latency and decision-record completeness (23) and case-workflow transition tests (27). Use synthetic, sanitized or explicitly authorized data only.

**Projects and assessment:** service charter and authority table (10), inventory and dependency model (11), evidence-flow matrix with canaries (12), tool acceptance tests (17), tested containment and timed restore (18), detection register (19), playbook comparison (24), team workflow studio (25), deployed workflow with pilot (27), authorized field simulation (28), the integrated capstone (29) and improvement dossier (30). Every exercise carries a 0–3 rubric.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX090 across the 30 chapters (three per chapter, matching the plan), with worked results in the final appendix of the same book. The draft exceeds the publication-gate minimum of 50 exercises.

**References and glossary:** verifiable primary sources and clear terms belong in the authored manuscript; linked project playbooks retain their source rights.
