## Thirty-chapter technologist route

This is the three-year **technologist degree** in Web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security. The book follows synthetic student **Lucas Moreau** of the fictional **Pinecrest Technical College**, guided by **Ms Grace Adebayo**, operating the fictional **Tidewater Makerspace**'s booking-credit token (about 350 members) on a three-node permissioned EVM ledger.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | Computing, networks, programming, data, security literacy, operating systems, identity, architecture, evidence, orientation | EX001–EX030 |
| 11–20 | Assets, data flows, risk scenarios, review methods, guided lab, tools, tooling, controls, monitoring, troubleshooting | lab_sync.py (15), lab_privlog.py (19); EX031–EX060 |
| 21–30 | Integration, automation, evaluation, research, team studio, handoff, deployment, field practice, capstone | lab_drift.py (22); EX061–EX090 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): node-c lags 5, 9 and 14 blocks at minutes 2–4 (threshold 2); 6 privileged events, 2 unmatched (admin grant at block 6012 with no ticket, upgrade v1.3 citing CHG-115); 15 settings checked, 3 drifted (node-b client 2.4.0; node-c public RPC and 30-day logs).

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no protocol is named and no attack steps are given.
- Labs are read-only, standard-library checks; none construct transactions or exploits.
