**Labs and practice:** seventeen offline Python labs (standard library only) in the manuscript, building the synthetic Fallowmere LANTERN telemetry service step by step up to the end-to-end monitored build in Chapter 29. Use synthetic, sanitized or explicitly authorized data. Do not probe or test real infrastructure.

**Projects and assessment:** integration project, operational evidence and design review (Chapters 25–30). The capstone rubric is in Chapter 30; every exercise carries a 0–3 rubric.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX090 across the thirty chapters (three per chapter), with worked results in the final appendix of the same book. The draft meets the publication-gate minimum of 50 and the technologist planning target of 90.

**References and glossary:** verifiable primary sources and clear terms belong in the authored manuscript; linked project playbooks retain their source rights.
