## Thirty-subject teaching route

This is the three-year **technologist course** for application and software supply chain security. The book follows synthetic technologist student **Diego Ramos** of the fictional **Southport Polytechnic**, on placement at the fictional web agency **Brightwave Digital** with mentor **Aiko Mori**. It starts with a three-day manual search for clients affected by an upload-kit advisory, which included **Greenleaf Veterinary Group**. From there it covers foundations, inventory, checks, pipeline controls, monitoring, automation, evaluation and handoff, and ends with a four-day field response and an SBOM capstone.
