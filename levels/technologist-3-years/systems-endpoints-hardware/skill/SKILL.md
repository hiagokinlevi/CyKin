## Thirty-subject teaching route

This is the three-year **technologist course** for systems, endpoint and hardware security. The book follows synthetic student **Tomás Herrera** of the fictional **Granite Coast College**, guided by **Renée Okafor**, on placement with IT technician **Dev Malik** at the fictional **Linden Schools Trust**, after a departed supply teacher's account was found still active.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | Scope, hardware and firmware, OS and privilege, Linux and Windows administration, networks, identity, scripting, principles, data protection | lab_stale.py (7); EX001–EX030 |
| 11–24 | Inventory, Linux and Windows baselines, OS/app/firmware patching, encryption, backups, logging, endpoint protection, application control, remote work, configuration management, incidents, hardware lifecycle | lab_sshcfg.py (12), lab_backupage.py (17); EX031–EX072 |
| 25–30 | Placement plan, baseline, improvements, result, routines, report | EX073–EX090 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 4 of 7 enabled accounts stale over 90 days; 2 of 4 Linux servers meet the SSH baseline (4 deviations); 2 of 6 servers' backups older than 24 h (mis01 70 h, print01 26 h). Project result: stale accounts 4→0, SSH 2→4 of 4, laptop baseline 31→93 of 94, encryption 87→94 of 94, backups on time 4→6 of 6.
