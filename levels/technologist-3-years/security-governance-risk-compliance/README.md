# Security governance, risk and compliance — Technologist course

**Level:** Technologist course, 3 years · **School:** School of Data and Security Governance · **Planned study:** approximately 2400 hours.

**Scope:** Security strategy, risk decisions, audit evidence, assurance, policy and program measurement.

**Audience:** Technology implementers and administrators. **Entry knowledge:** Computing foundations and introductory security operations.

**Exit goal:** integrate services, controls, operations and measurement in security governance, risk and compliance. **Practical work:** implement an end-to-end service with monitored controls. **Assessment plan:** integration project, operational evidence and design review.

This is one course. It has **one course skill** and **one principal book in each language edition**. Its **30 curricular subjects are the chapters of that book**. Every finished chapter must teach the subject, give worked examples and exercises; worked answers belong at the end of the same book. The 300-page A4 and 50-exercise requirements apply to a finished edition, not to the plans below.
