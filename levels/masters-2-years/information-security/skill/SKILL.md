## Eight-chapter master's route

This is the two-year **master's course** in information security. The book follows synthetic student **Priya Natarajan**, supervised by **Dr. Olumide Bello** at the fictional **Ashcombe University**, evaluating a phishing feedback pilot at the fictional **Harrowgate Community Health**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Literature method, standards seminar, design, ethics and reproducibility | EX001–EX020 |
| 4–6 | Rival explanations, measurement pilot and analysis | lab_clicks (5), lab_did (6); EX021–EX038 |
| 7–8 | Writing, limitations, independent review and defense | EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 1,200 synthetic staff: 239 raw clicks (19.9%), 77 within 2 s attributed to the scanner, 162 human clicks (13.5%); click rates 15.8% to 6.7% (feedback) vs 14.0% to 11.0% (comparison); difference-in-differences -6.2 points, 95% CI -10.7 to -1.2.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
