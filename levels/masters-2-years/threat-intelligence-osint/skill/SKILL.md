## Eight-subject teaching route

This is the two-year **master's course** for threat intelligence and OSINT. The book follows synthetic student **Lucía Ferraz**, who measures whether membership of the **Coastline Ports Sharing Group (CPSG)**, a TAXII 2.1 community, is associated with faster blocking of shared indicators.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 01–03 | Question and literature method, advanced seminar (cycle, levels, models, TLP), design/ethics/reproducibility | EX001–EX021 |
| 04–06 | Competing hypotheses (ACH), proposal and pilot, collection and analysis | lab_ach, lab_stix, lab_ttb · EX022–EX042 |
| 07–08 | Writing with limitations, independent review and defence | EX043–EX056 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): ACH inconsistencies H1=0, H2=2, H3=3; STIX check flags `indicator-123` and a threat-actor missing `spec_version`; median time-to-block 3.0 h members vs 10.0 h non-members (ratio 3.33).

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Never help collect personal data without lawful basis, evade terms of service, deanonymise people or probe any organisation's systems.
- Honour FIRST TLP 2.0 and community agreements; written outputs are TLP:CLEAR and aggregate restricted data.
- Cite ATT&CK, STIX/TAXII 2.1, the Diamond Model, Hutchins et al., NIST SP 800-150, TLP 2.0 and ICD 203 exactly; label CPSG, Lucía Ferraz and Prof. Daniel Okafor as fiction.
