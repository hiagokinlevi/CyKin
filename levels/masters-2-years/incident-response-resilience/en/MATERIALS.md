# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Master's course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons. The English draft covers all 8 chapters and follows one synthetic master's dissertation, the Triage Rubric Study (a randomized crossover tabletop at the fictional Marlow Regional Shared Services SOC), from question to defense.

**Labs and practice:** design and execute an ethically reviewed study. Eight offline Python labs (standard library only), one per chapter, with every expected output printed in the book: search deduplication, known-item recall and screening kappa (1); how success measures and censoring reverse a method comparison (2); design analysis of crossover versus parallel tabletops (3); rival explanations, difference-in-differences, standardization and a negative control on historical tickets (4); pilot audit against progression criteria (5); the main paired crossover analysis with bootstrap and permutation inference (6); number provenance (7); and a reviewer's specification curve (8). Labs 7 and 8 import the Chapter 6 files. Use synthetic, sanitized or explicitly authorized data only.

**Projects and assessment:** research proposal (Chapter 5), pilot report, reproducible results package (Chapter 6), dissertation with results-to-claims register and directional limitations (Chapter 7), and an independent review, mock defense and partner briefing (Chapter 8). Every exercise carries a 0–3 rubric.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX050 across the eight chapters (7, 7, 6, 6, 6, 6, 6, 6, matching the plan), with worked results in the final appendix of the same book. The draft meets the publication-gate minimum of 50 exercises.

**References and glossary:** verifiable primary sources and clear terms belong in the authored manuscript; linked project playbooks retain their source rights.
