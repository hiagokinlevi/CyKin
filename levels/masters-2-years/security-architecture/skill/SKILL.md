## Eight-chapter master's route

This is the two-year **master's course** in security architecture and engineering. The book follows synthetic student **Kenji Albrecht**, supervised by **Dr Selin Aydın** at the fictional **Northmoor University**, simulating segmentation strategies on networks shaped like the fictional **Vantor Logistics**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Question and recorded search, three architecture views and principles, simulation design, ethics and reproducibility | lab_screen.py (1), lab_reach.py (3); EX001–EX020 |
| 4–6 | Rival explanations and overturn thresholds, proposal and pilot, bootstrap analysis and rival checks | lab_compare.py (6); EX021–EX038 |
| 7–8 | Writing and limitations, independent review and defense | EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 312 records screened to 27 included; reach from ws-fin1 flat 6, zone 4, identity 1, identity with broad jump host 6; zone minus identity 2.43 [2.29, 2.56], control minus identity 1.56 [1.42, 1.71]; mean rules zone 22.2 vs identity 67.7.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they model, count and check, and never probe real systems. Architecture advice must cite real standards and name its limits.
- Test or review only systems with written authorisation; report found secrets by location, never by value; never present a framework as proof of outcomes.
