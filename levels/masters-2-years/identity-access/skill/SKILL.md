## Eight-chapter master's route

This is the two-year **master's research track** for identity and access management. The book follows synthetic student **Jonah Reyes** of the fictional **Marlow Bay University**, supervised by **Dr Ingrid Solberg**, as he measures whether passkey enrolment at the fictional **Halyard Insurance** reduces password resets without lowering sign-in success.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Question and literature method; assurance, passkeys and federation; design, ethics and reproducibility | lab_screen.py (1); EX001–EX020 |
| 4–6 | Competing explanations; proposal and pilot; data collection and analysis | lab_rate.py (4), lab_success.py (6); EX021–EX038 |
| 7–8 | Writing and limitations; independent review and viva | EX039–EX050 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 12 records screened to 6; resets 4.59 → 2.42 per 100 staff (−47%); sign-in success 97.6% vs 99.2% with non-overlapping Wilson intervals.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic aggregate data only; no phishing, credential attacks or bypass steps.
- The researcher never accesses partner systems; partners supply pseudonymised aggregates.
