# Principal book plan — OT, IoT and cyber-physical security / Master's course / English

**One book for this course and language.** 8 curricular subjects are its chapters. Duration: 2 years; planned effort: approximately 1200 hours. Audience: Beginning researchers. Entry knowledge: Strong domain foundations and research-method literacy. Intended outcome: formulate a question, evaluate prior work and produce reproducible findings in ot, iot and cyber-physical security.

**Editorial state:** structured outline; manuscript not yet authored. English reference edition.

## Book requirements

A finished book must contain at least 300 actual A4 pages and 50 substantive exercises with worked results. The current 600-page budget is a plan, not measured pages. Every chapter needs teaching, definitions, a worked example, practical activity, errors and limits, exercises and a synthesis. The final appendix holds answers keyed to the exercise IDs. Cite verifiable sources, label synthetic cases, and review for technical accuracy, rights and accessibility.

## Practical and assessment plan

Practice: design and execute an ethically reviewed study. Assessment: research proposal, reproducible results and dissertation. Allocate at least one authored exercise to every chapter and at least 50 in the whole book. Each technical exercise needs a goal, inputs, authorized scope, steps, expected evidence and a worked or rubric-based result. The final book requires a glossary, references and an A4 rendering verified under the [book standard](../../../../../BOOK_STANDARD.md).
