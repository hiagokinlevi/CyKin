## Forty-chapter bachelor's route

This is the four-year **bachelor's degree** in Web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security. The book follows synthetic student **Sofia Lindqvist** of the fictional **Harrowgate University**, supervised by **Dr Kwame Asante**, helping the fictional **Lantern Hill Food Cooperative** (about 900 members) run a voucher token on a permissioned EVM test ledger with three validators and a 2-of-3 treasury.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | Reasoning, computing, programming, writing, security literacy, data, ethics | EX001–EX030 |
| 11–20 | Operating systems, statistics, identity, privacy, evidence, architecture, cryptography, research, scope and foundational cases | lab_digest.py (17); EX031–EX060 |
| 21–30 | Models, dependencies, review methods, controls, verification, failure analysis, operations | lab_events.py (25), lab_config.py (30); EX061–EX090 |
| 31–40 | Advanced cases, interfaces, research, comparison, capstone and thesis | EX091–EX120 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 4 voucher records, 1 changed (V002), 64-hex SHA-256 digests; 6 events replay to supply 110 vs snapshot 115, mismatch m04 45/50; configuration check runs 4 checks, 2 issues (mint timelock 24 < 48, tests 41/42).

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no protocol is named and no attack steps are given.
- Labs are read-only, standard-library checks; none construct transactions or exploits.
