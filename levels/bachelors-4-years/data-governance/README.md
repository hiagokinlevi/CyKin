# Data governance — Bachelor's course

**Level:** Bachelor's course, 4 years · **School:** School of Data and Security Governance · **Planned study:** approximately 3200 hours.

**Scope:** Data ownership, quality, lineage, lifecycle, stewardship, access decisions and accountable use.

**Audience:** Broad security and computing students. **Entry knowledge:** Mathematics, computing and communication foundations.

**Exit goal:** compare theories and methods, evaluate evidence and design defensible solutions in data governance. **Practical work:** compare alternatives using explicit assumptions and data. **Assessment plan:** research paper, cross-domain capstone and oral defense.

This is one course. It has **one course skill** and **one principal book in each language edition**. Its **40 curricular subjects are the chapters of that book**. Every finished chapter must teach the subject, give worked examples and exercises; worked answers belong at the end of the same book. The 300-page A4 and 50-exercise requirements apply to a finished edition, not to the plans below.
