# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Bachelor's course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons. The English draft covers all 40 chapters and uses one synthetic organization, the Harbourline Health Cooperative (a regional clinic network), across foundations, discipline chapters and the capstone, plus clearly labelled summaries of two public Cyber Safety Review Board reports in Chapter 20.

**Labs and practice:** compare alternatives using explicit assumptions and data. Twelve offline Python labs (standard library only) with every expected output printed in the book: intake validation (3), access-graph reachability and revocation invariants (6), JSON alert parsing and deduplication with provenance (8), joins and ambiguous matches in SQLite (9), detection measures, base rates and Simpson's paradox (12), missing-timestamp bounds (15), expected-harm containment with sensitivity (24), multi-source timeline with clock-offset estimation (25), severity-label drift (29), before/after versus difference-in-differences (33), a gated case workflow (38) and capstone evaluation (39). Use synthetic, sanitized or explicitly authorized data only.

**Projects and assessment:** comparative strategy report (Chapter 34), capstone proposal with ethics checklist (35), supervised community tabletop with consent letter and reflection (36), case-critique seminar (37), capstone implementation, evaluation and thesis with oral defense (38–40). Every exercise carries a 0–3 rubric.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX120 across the 40 chapters (three per chapter, matching the plan), with worked results in the final appendix of the same book. The draft meets the publication-gate minimum of 50 exercises.

**References and glossary:** verifiable primary sources and clear terms belong in the authored manuscript; linked project playbooks retain their source rights.
