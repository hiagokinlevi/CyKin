## Forty-chapter bachelor's route

This is the four-year **bachelor's course** in privacy and data protection. The book follows synthetic student **Elena Rossi**, tutored by **Dr. Kwame Asante** at the fictional **Fairhaven College**, improving data protection at the fictional **Brindle Valley Schools Trust**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | Reasoning, systems, programming, writing, security literacy, structures, networks, data and ethics | lab_sqlite (9); EX001–EX030 |
| 11–20 | Operating systems, statistics, identity, privacy foundations, measurement, architecture, cryptography, research and cases | lab_wilson (12), lab_sar (15); EX031–EX060 |
| 21–30 | System models, app dependencies, disclosure control, controls, laboratories, consent, verification, failure analysis and operations | lab_vet (22), lab_cells (23), lab_photo (26); EX061–EX090 |
| 31–40 | Hard cases, interdisciplinary work, research II, comparison, capstone portal, community practice, evidence review and thesis | EX091–EX120 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): three apps without agreements (5,800 pupil-app shares), ReadAlong most serious; awareness 25% (22–29%), Ashby 22% vs Kelso 46%; access requests median 26 days, 6 of 20 late; vetting approves MathsQuest 10 and Timetabler 8; 4 of 8 table cells suppressed; 2 of 3 photographs held; portal emailed reports ~40 to 3 a month, activation 83% vs 85% target.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal analysis here is teaching, not legal advice, and a synthetic DPIA or audit is never presented as a real regulatory decision.
