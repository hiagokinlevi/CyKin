# Principal book plan — Privacy and data protection / Bachelor's course / עברית

**One book for this course and language.** 40 curricular subjects are its chapters. Duration: 4 years; planned effort: approximately 3200 hours. Audience: Broad security and computing students. Entry knowledge: Mathematics, computing and communication foundations. Intended outcome: compare theories and methods, evaluate evidence and design defensible solutions in privacy and data protection.

**Editorial state:** structured outline; manuscript not yet authored. English working summaries await translation and review.

[Home](../../../../../README.md) · [Course home](../../README.md) · [Edition](../README.md) · [Chapter index](../SUBJECTS.md) · [Course skill](../../skill/SKILL.md)

## Book requirements

A finished book must contain at least 300 actual A4 pages and 50 substantive exercises with worked results. The current 1200-page budget is a plan, not measured pages. Every chapter needs teaching, definitions, a worked example, practical activity, errors and limits, exercises and a synthesis. The final appendix holds answers keyed to the exercise IDs. Cite verifiable sources, label synthetic cases, and review for technical accuracy, rights and accessibility.

## Practical and assessment plan

Practice: compare alternatives using explicit assumptions and data. Assessment: research paper, cross-domain capstone and oral defense. Allocate at least one authored exercise to every chapter and at least 50 in the whole book. Each technical exercise needs a goal, inputs, authorized scope, steps, expected evidence and a worked or rubric-based result. The final book requires a glossary, references and an A4 rendering verified under the [book standard](../../../../../BOOK_STANDARD.md).
