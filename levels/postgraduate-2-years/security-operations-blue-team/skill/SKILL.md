## Twelve-subject teaching route

This is the two-year **postgraduate course** for security operations and blue team. The book follows synthetic SOC team lead **Daniel Okoro** of the fictional **Meridian Health Insurance**, studying at the fictional **Hollins College** with tutor **Prof. Miriam Achebe**: from a night-time contractor-account export incident, through insider-risk evidence, coverage design and controlled evaluation, to an applied project that cuts time to contain compromised contractor accounts.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Decision-making under uncertainty, current practice, specialist evidence, risk and ethics | lab_decision.py (1), lab_peer.py (3); EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Design alternatives, controlled evaluation, organisational integration, difficult cases | lab_bootstrap.py (6); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Leadership and assurance, research critique, applied project, final review | lab_assurance.py (9); EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): expected costs 11.6k / 42.4k / 180.0k at p = 0.30, monitoring best at p = 0.05; h04 ratio 3.07 to peer median; median triage 24.5 → 17.5 min, bootstrap interval [3.5, 11.0]; P1 within 15 min 85% → 95% (March 79%), tested coverage 52% → 68%.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Synthetic telemetry only; no exploit code, payloads, attack procedures or evasion guidance.
- Standard-library labs; insider-risk monitoring only under agreed policy with HR, legal and staff representatives.
- Test scenarios use dedicated test accounts with written approval.
