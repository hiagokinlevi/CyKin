## Twelve-chapter postgraduate route

This is the two-year **postgraduate specialisation** in identity and access management. The book follows synthetic engineer **Marta Kowalczyk** of the fictional **Ashdown Graduate School**, supervised by **Prof. Leila Haddad**, as she redesigns privileged access at the fictional **Corran Logistics**: named administrator accounts, phishing-resistant keys, just-in-time elevation, vaulted emergency credentials and monitored activity.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Decision-making, current practice, standing-privilege evidence, risk and ethics | lab_standing.py (3); EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Design alternatives, controlled pilot, organisational integration, difficult cases | lab_score.py (5), lab_window.py (6); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Board assurance, research critique, applied rollout, final review and transfer | EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): standing 1,344 vs needed 18.5 privilege-hours per week (−98.6%); design scores B 2.35, A 2.30, C 1.70; 3 of 10 activations outside change windows.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no credential attacks, privilege escalation techniques or bypass steps.
- Monitoring of administrators is action-focused, transparent and purpose-limited.
