## Twelve-subject teaching route

This is the **postgraduate course** for systems, endpoint and hardware security. The book follows synthetic student **Priya Natarajan** of the fictional **Westmere Polytechnic**, supervised by **Dr Owen Hale**, through an applied project at the fictional **Kestrel Dental Group**, where unsupported imaging workstations were found in an audit.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Risk framing, inventory, vendor engagement, exceptions with compensating controls | lab_eol.py (2), lab_exceptions.py (4); EX001–EX020 |
| 5–9 | Desktop baseline, patching, encryption, logs, recovery | EX021–EX045 |
| 10–12 | Governance, evaluation, presentation | EX046–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 12 of 60 workstations (20%) on an unsupported OS, none at Vale; exception register of 6 with 2 expired (IMG-N2, IMG-H2) and 1 lacking a compensating control (IMG-H1).

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no exploit code, payloads or bypass steps.
- Risk acceptance belongs to a named business owner; every exception expires.
