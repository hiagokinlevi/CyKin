# Cybersecurity — Postgraduate course

**Level:** Postgraduate course, 2 years · **School:** School of Cybersecurity Foundations · **Planned study:** approximately 600 hours.

**Scope:** Foundations, threats, controls, defense lifecycle, evidence and authorized practice across digital systems.

**Audience:** Experienced specialists. **Entry knowledge:** Prior degree or equivalent professional experience in the field.

**Exit goal:** lead advanced application and justify organization-scale choices in cybersecurity. **Practical work:** critique a real-world design with sanitized evidence. **Assessment plan:** advanced case analysis and applied final project.

This is one course. It has **one course skill** and **one principal book in each language edition**. Its **12 curricular subjects are the chapters of that book**. Every finished chapter must teach the subject, give worked examples and exercises; worked answers belong at the end of the same book. The 300-page A4 and 50-exercise requirements apply to a finished edition, not to the plans below.
