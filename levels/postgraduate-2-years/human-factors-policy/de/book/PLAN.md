# Principal book plan — Human factors and cyber policy / Postgraduate course / Deutsch

**One book for this course and language.** 12 curricular subjects are its chapters. Duration: 2 years; planned effort: approximately 600 hours. Audience: Experienced specialists. Entry knowledge: Prior degree or equivalent professional experience in the field. Intended outcome: lead advanced application and justify organization-scale choices in human factors and cyber policy.

**Editorial state:** structured outline; manuscript not yet authored. English working summaries await translation and review.

## Book requirements

A finished book must contain at least 300 actual A4 pages and 50 substantive exercises with worked results. The current 420-page budget is a plan, not measured pages. Every chapter needs teaching, definitions, a worked example, practical activity, errors and limits, exercises and a synthesis. The final appendix holds answers keyed to the exercise IDs. Cite verifiable sources, label synthetic cases, and review for technical accuracy, rights and accessibility.

### Chapter 01 — Advanced security decision-making

**Stage 1; 35 planned A4 pages; 5 planned exercises.** Planned; authored chapter pending.

This subject teaches **Advanced security decision-making** and contributes to the postgraduate course outcome: lead advanced application and justify organization-scale choices in human factors and cyber policy. The chapter will define the underlying terms and map the evidence and decisions that depend on them. At this level, learners will critique a real-world design with sanitized evidence. Its worked example will use a sourced overview or clearly labeled synthetic service record. The learner will complete a concept check and bounded evidence analysis and at least 5 exercises, then compare reasoning with the final worked-answer appendix. It builds on the entry knowledge and prepares Chapter 02. This paragraph is an editorial summary, not an authored lesson.

## Practical and assessment plan

Practice: critique a real-world design with sanitized evidence. Assessment: advanced case analysis and applied final project. Allocate at least one authored exercise to every chapter and at least 50 in the whole book. Each technical exercise needs a goal, inputs, authorized scope, steps, expected evidence and a worked or rubric-based result. The final book requires a glossary, references and an A4 rendering verified under the [book standard](../../../../../BOOK_STANDARD.md).
