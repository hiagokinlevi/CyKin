## Twelve-subject teaching route

This is the two-year **postgraduate course** for application and software supply chain security. The book follows synthetic product security lead **Hannah Brooks** of the fictional **Northbridge Bank**, studying at the fictional **Calder College** with tutor **Dr Ravi Menon**: from an unreviewed analytics SDK update that sent device location to a vendor, through decisions, specialist evidence, design, controlled evaluation, integration and assurance, to an applied project that cut SDKs and detection time.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Decision-making, current practice, specialist evidence, risk and ethics | lab_decision.py (1), lab_prioritise.py (3); EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Design alternatives, controlled evaluation, organisational integration, difficult cases | lab_eval.py (6); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Leadership and assurance, research critique, applied project, final review | lab_assurance.py (9); EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): expected costs 270k / 132k / 238k (review on update lowest); CVSS order F1, F5, F3, F2, F4 vs risk order F2, F4, F3, F5, F1; detection median 2 vs 11.5 days, difference 9.5, 95% interval 6.0–19.0 (seed 3); provenance verified 64% → 81% → 100%, SDK reviews 68% → 87% → 100%.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Synthetic code and data only; no exploit code, payloads, attack procedures or instructions for bypassing controls.
- Standard-library labs; SDK behaviour is observed only in test harnesses with synthetic data.
- Evaluations are approved, reported by team, and never use customer data.
