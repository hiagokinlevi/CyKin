# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Postgraduate course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons. The English draft covers all 12 chapters and follows the fictional multi-division Calder Group through difficult incidents and an incident-program redesign; Chapter 10 critiques the methods of public industry reports (IBM/Ponemon, Mandiant M-Trends, Verizon DBIR) without relying on their headline figures.

**Labs and practice:** lead advanced application with explicit decision records. Four offline Python labs (standard library only) with expected outputs printed in the book: appetite-weighted option comparison with robustness across uncertainties (1), restore candidates evaluated against an independent reference (6), a hypothesis–evidence matrix with diagnosticity (8) and a handoff pilot with a concurrent comparison (11). Use synthetic, sanitized or explicitly authorized data only.

**Projects and assessment:** command charter and stress test (2), reconciled specialist evidence with confidence statement (3), proportionality review record (4), coordinated containment plan (5), legal–privacy–operations decision log (7), executive and board briefings (9), and the applied program-redesign project with pilot evaluation and transfer package (11–12). Every exercise carries a 0–3 rubric.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX060 across the 12 chapters (five per chapter, matching the plan), with worked results in the final appendix of the same book. The draft meets the publication-gate minimum of 50 exercises.

**References and glossary:** verifiable primary sources and clear terms belong in the authored manuscript; linked project playbooks retain their source rights.
