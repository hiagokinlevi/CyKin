## Twelve-chapter postgraduate route

This is the two-year **postgraduate diploma** in Web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security. The book follows synthetic risk manager **Nadia Rahman** of the fictional **Westbrook Institute**, supervised by **Professor Mei Tanaka**, as the fictional **Harbourside Regional Credit Union** and two partners pilot tokenised-deposit settlement on a shared permissioned ledger.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Decision-making, current practice, specialist evidence | EX001–EX015 |
| 4–6 | Risk and ethics, design alternatives, controlled evaluation | lab_signoff.py (6); EX016–EX030 |
| 7–9 | Integration, difficult cases, leadership and assurance | EX031–EX045 |
| 10–12 | Research critique, applied project, final review | lab_quorum.py (11); EX046–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): sign-off design A catches 1/4 seeded errors (median 6.0 min), design B 4/4 (median 11.5 min); 3-of-4 quorum unavailable 3/720 hours, availability 99.58%.
