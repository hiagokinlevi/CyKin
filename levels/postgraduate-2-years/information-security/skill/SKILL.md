## Twelve-chapter postgraduate route

This is the two-year **postgraduate course** in information security. The book follows synthetic practitioner **Elena Petrova**, taught by **Dr. Hamid Qureshi** at the fictional **Thornbury Business School**, while securing the fictional **Moorfield Dental Group**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Case, governance and management system, risk assessment and inventory | EX001–EX020 |
| 5–8 | Patching, identity and access, phishing and recovery | lab_patch (5), lab_lookalike (7); EX021–EX040 |
| 9–12 | Incidents, board reporting, capstone and presentation | EX041–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 566 synthetic devices: 513 (90.6%) patched within 14 days; lowest clinics C19 10/20 (50.0%), C15 20/28 (71.4%), C07 22/30 (73.3%); 5,823 synthetic messages from 68 domains: 4 lookalike domains within edit distance 2 sent 23 messages.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
