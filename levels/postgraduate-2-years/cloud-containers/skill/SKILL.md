## Twelve-subject teaching route

This is the two-year **postgraduate course** for cloud and container security. The book follows synthetic cloud security lead **Inês Carvalho** of the fictional insurer **Beacon Mutual**, studying at the fictional **Fenwick Graduate School** with tutor **Prof. Sunita Rao**. After a Kubernetes 1.25 upgrade removed PodSecurityPolicy, Pod Security admission was not configured and four clusters ran three months without enforcement.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Decision, context, evidence | lab_decision.py (1), lab_evidence.py (3); EX001–EX015 |
| 4–6 | Risk and ethics, design alternatives, controlled evaluation | lab_eval.py (6); EX016–EX030 |
| 7–9 | Integration, difficult cases, leadership and assurance | lab_assurance.py (9); EX031–EX045 |
| 10–12 | Research critique, applied project, final review | EX046–EX060 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): option costs A 130k, B 131k, C 237k; 4/11 namespaces enforced (36%); policy set P1 recall 0.75, precision 0.64, P2 recall 0.92, precision 0.96 on 200 deployments (24 risky); assurance pass 6/15, 11/15, 15/15 with exceptions 21, 9, 4.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Synthetic configurations, records and data only; no exploit code, payloads, attack procedures or instructions for bypassing controls.
- Standard-library labs that only read synthetic records.
- Findings are framed as process gaps; individuals are not blamed or assessed.
