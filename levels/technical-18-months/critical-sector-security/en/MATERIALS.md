# Materials — Critical infrastructure and sector security / Technical course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/critical-sector-security/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons. The English draft covers all 18 chapters.

**Labs and practice:** fifteen offline Python labs (standard library only) in the manuscript — Modbus/TCP and DNP3 decoding, host auditing, strict data validation, clock-corrected timelines, failure-mode rules, firewall evaluation, verification traceability, a contained Modbus responder (Chapter 12), hypothesis elimination, drift detection, dependency analysis, a detection pipeline (Chapter 17) and an evidence seal (Chapter 18). Use synthetic, sanitized or explicitly authorized data. Do not probe or test real infrastructure.

**Projects and assessment:** practical examinations (contained laboratories in Chapters 12 and 17, each with a rubric), troubleshooting log (Chapter 13) and supervised project with a sealed evidence package (Chapter 18). Every exercise carries a 0–3 rubric.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX072 across the eighteen chapters (four per chapter), with worked results in the final appendix of the same book. The draft meets the publication-gate minimum of 50 and the planned target of four per chapter.

**References and glossary:** verifiable primary sources and clear terms belong in the authored manuscript; linked project playbooks retain their source rights.
