## Eighteen-chapter technical route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** in information security. The book follows synthetic student **Chloe Martin**, tutored by **Mr. Kwesi Appiah** at the fictional **Harborview Technical College**, on placement with the fictional **Westfield Garden Centres**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | Case, information, devices and accounts, passwords, Python checks and security basics | EX001–EX024 |
| 7–12 | Updates, phishing, personal data, data security, wireless and backups | lab_updates (7), lab_phish (8); EX025–EX048 |
| 13–18 | Reporting, documentation, working with the IT lead, placement, assessment and review | EX049–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 64 synthetic devices: tills 0 of 22, laptops 2 of 18 (11.1%), tablets 4 of 24 (16.7%) not updated for over 30 days, 6 in total; 40 synthetic emails (12 phishing): a two-sign rule catches 11, misses 1 (sender mismatch only) and raises 3 false alarms.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
