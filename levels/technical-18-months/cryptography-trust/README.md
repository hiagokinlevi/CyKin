# Cryptography and trust services — Technical course

**Level:** Technical course, 18 months · **School:** School of Secure Engineering · **Planned study:** approximately 1200 hours.

**Scope:** Cryptographic principles, protocols, key management, certificates and trust infrastructure.

**Audience:** Technical operators. **Entry knowledge:** Networks, operating systems, scripting and safe lab practice.

**Exit goal:** diagnose failures, configure controls and verify outcomes in cryptography and trust services. **Practical work:** build and troubleshoot a reproducible lab. **Assessment plan:** practical examinations, troubleshooting log and supervised project.

This is one course. It has **one course skill** and **one principal book in each language edition**. Its **18 curricular subjects are the chapters of that book**. Every finished chapter must teach the subject, give worked examples and exercises; worked answers belong at the end of the same book. The 300-page A4 and 50-exercise requirements apply to a finished edition, not to the plans below.
