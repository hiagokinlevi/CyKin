## Eighteen-subject teaching route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** for cloud and container security. The book follows synthetic apprentice **Tomás Reyes** of the fictional **Hollins Technical College**, supervised by **Grace Mensah** at the fictional courier **Fairway Logistics**, after a delivery-manifests bucket was made public to share a file with a partner.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | Services, networks, identities, scripting, scope and evidence, system model | EX001–EX024 |
| 7–12 | Telemetry, failure modes, methods, configuration, verification, contained lab I | lab_events.py (7), lab_verify.py (11), lab_secrets.py (12); EX025–EX048 |
| 13–18 | Troubleshooting, automation, integration, documentation, contained lab II, project | lab_drift.py (17); EX049–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): bucket public from 3 March 16:41 to 6 March 10:02 with 2 anonymous reads; 6 policy differences across 4 buckets (driver-photos worst); 3 secret-like keys in 2 of 4 files; 3 of 7 settings drifted (logging, public endpoint, replicas).

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Synthetic configurations, records and data only; no exploit code, payloads, attack procedures or instructions for bypassing controls.
- Standard-library labs that only read synthetic records and never print secret values.
- Reviews are blameless; work stays inside a written, read-only scope under supervision.
