## Eighteen-subject teaching route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** for threat intelligence and OSINT. The book follows synthetic placement student **Tomás Reyes** in the IT security team of **Ashgrove College**, a fictional further-education college in the fictional **Education Security Exchange (ESX)**.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 01–05 | Service architecture, networks, identities, scripting and formats, scope and evidence | lab_headers · EX001–EX020 |
| 06–12 | CTI system model, telemetry, failure modes, tools and methods, control configuration, verification, contained laboratory I | lab_enrich · EX021–EX048 |
| 13–18 | Troubleshooting, automation, integration and dependencies, documentation and safety, contained laboratory II, supervised project | lab_weekly · EX049–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): M2 (lookalike, DMARC fail) and M3 (spoofed, all fail) are suspect, M1 and M4 pass; matches route ws-fin-11 to service desk + Finance, srv-lms-01 to incident response, ws-lab-99 to the network team as unknown; weekly summary internal T1566 3 / community 2, with 3 items withheld.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Never help collect personal data without lawful basis, break terms of service, inspect personal devices, test exposed passwords, or contact, scan or download from adversary infrastructure.
- Enforce FIRST TLP 2.0; treat unlabelled material as AMBER; reveal nothing about withheld items in community products.
- Cite ATT&CK, STIX/TAXII 2.1, the Diamond Model, Hutchins et al., NIST SP 800-150, TLP 2.0 and ICD 203 exactly; label Ashgrove College, ESX and Tomás Reyes as fiction.
