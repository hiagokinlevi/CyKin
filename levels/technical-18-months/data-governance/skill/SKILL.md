## Eighteen-chapter technical route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** in data governance. The book follows synthetic student **Marta Kowalski**, trained by **Mr. Daniel Osei** at the fictional **Southport Technical College**, working with the fictional **Linkway Buses**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | Case, bus company data, forms, spreadsheets, Python checks and quality basics | EX001–EX024 |
| 7–12 | Missing counts, stop names, personal data, security, sharing and retention | lab_trips (7), lab_stops (8); EX025–EX048 |
| 13–18 | Problem reports, documentation, stewardship, placement, assessment and review | EX049–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): Trips without counts: Route 1 12 (1.0%), Route 4 10 (0.8%), Route 7 122 (10.2%); 144 of 3,600 overall (4.0%); 12 stop names normalise to 5 distinct stops.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
