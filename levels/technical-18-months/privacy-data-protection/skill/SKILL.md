## Eighteen-chapter technical route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** in privacy and data protection. The book follows synthetic student **Leila Haddad**, taught by **Mr. Daniel Okafor** at the fictional **Riverside Technical College**, on placement at the fictional **Marlow Dental Group**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | Services, networks, identities, scripting and data formats, scope and evidence | EX001–EX020 |
| 6–11 | Processing model, telemetry, failure modes, methods, consent configuration and verification | lab_logs (7), lab_cookies (10); EX021–EX044 |
| 12–18 | Contained labs, troubleshooting, retention automation, integration, documentation and a supervised project | lab_access (12), lab_retention (14); EX045–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): 95 of 400 synthetic log lines (23.8%) held personal data and none after masking; 3 of 7 cookies were set before consent without exemption; 179 of 5,060 record views (3.5%) were outside the staff member's clinic, with one account at 53; 712 of 1,200 reminder records (59.3%) were past 365 days, the oldest 900 days.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal analysis here is teaching, not legal advice, and a synthetic DPIA or audit is never presented as a real regulatory decision.
