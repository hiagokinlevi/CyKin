## Eighteen-subject teaching route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** for application and software supply chain security. The book follows synthetic technical student **Tomás Rivera** of the fictional **Eastbank Technical College**, a support technician at the fictional **Pinecrest Hosting** working under supervisor **Grace Okafor**. It starts when an image-resize plugin, installed for the customer **Ferncroft Books** from an unverified mirror, causes unexpected redirects. From there it covers foundations, the supply chain model, telemetry, failure modes, inventory, baseline configuration, verification, contained labs, automation, dependencies and documentation, and ends with a supervised project.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | Architecture, networks, identities, scripting and hashes, scope and evidence, system model | lab_hash.py (4); EX001–EX024 |
| 7–12 | Telemetry, failure modes, tools, control configuration, verification, contained lab I | lab_logs.py (7), lab_headers.py (10), lab_versions.py (12); EX025–EX048 |
| 13–18 | Troubleshooting, automation, dependencies, documentation and safety, contained lab II, supervised project | EX049–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): the official resize and gallery zips MATCH, while the mirror zip is a MISMATCH (5af89d46d94f); hour 10 has 5 errors and hour 11 has 2, and 198.51.100.7 has 5 failed admin sign-ins (REVIEW); ferncroft-books passes the header check, alder-dental lacks CSP and Referrer-Policy, and quayside-gym lacks HSTS and shows a version banner; the advisory totals are high 1 and medium 2.
