## Eighteen-subject teaching route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** for systems, endpoint and hardware security. The book follows synthetic student **Nadia Petrova** of the fictional **Brookfield Technical Institute**, taught by **Sam Adeyinka**, on placement with office manager **Lena Brooks** at the fictional accountancy firm **Harlow & Pike**, after a free tool installed with admin rights brought adware.

| Subjects | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | Support role, hardware and firmware, accounts and rights, passwords and MFA, updates, protection software | lab_admins.py (3), lab_updates.py (5); EX001–EX024 |
| 7–15 | Safe installation, Windows settings, BitLocker, backups, networks, remote access, phishing, device lifecycle, documentation | EX025–EX060 |
| 16–18 | Placement measuring, changes and results, reflection | EX061–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 4 of 10 sampled PCs with extra local admins (5 accounts, incl. temp); 25 of 30 PCs updated within 14 days, 5 late, oldest 60 days. Project result: PCs with extra admins 12→0 of 30; updates within 14 days 25→29 of 30.
