## Eighteen-chapter technical route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** in Web3, blockchain and cryptoasset security. The book follows synthetic trainee **Aiko Brennan** of the fictional **Northfield Technical Institute**, mentored by **Mr Tomasz Kowal**, supporting the fictional **Greenway Tool Library** (about 600 borrowers), whose refundable deposits are tokens on a three-node permissioned EVM ledger.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | Service architecture, networks, identities, scripting, scope and evidence, system model | EX001–EX024 |
| 7–12 | Telemetry, failure modes, tools, control configuration, verification, contained lab I | lab_backup.py (12); EX025–EX048 |
| 13–18 | Troubleshooting, automation, dependencies, documentation, contained lab II, supervised project | lab_rate.py (17); EX049–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): backups node1 ok, node2 digest mismatch, node3 age 52 h > 24 h; transfer baseline mean 13.5, sd 1.58, limit 18.2, hour 12 flagged at 41.
