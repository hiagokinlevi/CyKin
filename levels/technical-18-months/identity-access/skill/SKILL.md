## Eighteen-chapter technical route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** in identity and access management. The book follows synthetic trainee **Tomas Novak** of the fictional **Ridgeway Technical College**, supervised by **Priya Raman**, as he configures, verifies and documents access control at the fictional **Millbrook Dental Practice** (two surgeries, 28 staff): MFA policies, role groups, lifecycle steps, separated admin accounts, single sign-on, reviews and recovery.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | Identity services, protocols, devices, scripting, directory quality, MFA configuration | EX001–EX024 |
| 7–12 | Groups and roles, lifecycle, stale accounts, privileged and service accounts, logs and alerts, MFA coverage | lab_stale.py (9), lab_mfa.py (12); EX025–EX048 |
| 13–18 | Single sign-on, troubleshooting, access reviews, recovery, documentation, supervised project | EX049–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): 10 accounts, 9 enabled; stale c.dunn (149 days), d.reyes (198), temp-01 (120); ownerless d.reyes and temp-01; MFA 23/28 (82%), phishing-resistant 6/28 (21%), reception 5/8 (62%).
