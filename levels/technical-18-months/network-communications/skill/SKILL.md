## Eighteen-chapter technical route

This is the eighteen-month **technical course** for network and communications security. The book follows synthetic trainee **Amina Yusuf** of the fictional **Harrowgate Technical Institute**, taught by **Leon Fischer**, working under the IT officer of the fictional **Castleford Housing Association** to separate zones, restrict outbound traffic, verify VPN and TLS settings, track certificates, automate read-only checks and verify backups.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | Services, protocols, hosts and identities, scripting, scope and evidence, system model | EX001–EX024 |
| 7–12 | Telemetry, failure modes, tools and methods, control configuration, verification, contained lab I | lab_vpnlog.py (7), lab_egress.py (10), lab_certs.py (12); EX025–EX048 |
| 13–18 | Troubleshooting, automation, integration, documentation and safety, contained lab II, supervised project | lab_restore.py (17); EX049–EX072 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic): accounts "admin" (7 failures) and m.ortiz (6) exceed the threshold of 5; 4 of 8 outbound flows allowed; one certificate expired 9 days ago, one due in 19 days, two within 60 days; backups: 2 verify, 1 mismatch, 1 missing, 1 unlisted.

## Teaching guardrails

- Synthetic data only; no exploit code, payloads, attack traffic, scanning or bypass steps.
- Real-system tasks only with the IT officer's approval; labs stay isolated from real networks.
