**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons.

**Teaching and case:** the [English manuscript](book/MANUSCRIPT.md) drafts Chapters 1–3 of 18. Its fictional Lumen Library Reservations case builds a service dependency graph, reads a synthetic DNS/TCP/TLS/HTTP trace, and distinguishes host process, service principal, session and human attribution. The remaining 15 subjects have plans only.

**Labs and practice:** three offline, reproducible Python fixtures check dependency closure, a supplied network-chain interpretation and a literal session-key join. They access no network or real account; manual reasoning produces the same expected results.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX012 provide four artifacts per drafted chapter and [12 matching worked results in the same book](book/MANUSCRIPT.md#worked-results). The planned technical volume needs 18 chapters, 72 allocated exercises, substantive expansion and A4 and editorial review.

**Sources and rights:** NIST and RFC sources support general technical concepts; every LLR record is fictional and uses documentation-only names and addresses. Source documents retain their own rights.
