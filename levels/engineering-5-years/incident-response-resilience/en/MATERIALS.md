# Materials — Incident response and cyber resilience / Engineering course / English

[Edition overview](README.md) · [Chapters](SUBJECTS.md) · [One principal book plan](book/PLAN.md) · [Supplementary playbooks](../../../../playbooks/incident-response-resilience/README.md) · [Course skill](../skill/SKILL.md) · [Authored manuscript draft](book/MANUSCRIPT.md)

**Teaching and examples:** each subject-chapter belongs to the one principal book. Plans describe intended content; only the linked manuscript contains authored lessons. The English draft covers all 50 chapters and follows one synthetic organisation, Harrow Valley Shared Services (HVSS), as its response capability is specified, built, verified, trialled and defended.

**Labs and practice:** eighteen offline Python labs (standard library only) with every expected output printed in the book: incident-queue backlog (1), approval-graph invariants (2), quarantining intake parser (4), intervals and Simpson's paradox (7), restoration order versus RPO (13), automated-containment feedback loop (17), anchored evidence manifest (19), fault tree of silent evidence loss (23), ranking with dropped fields (24), scoped containment control (27), evidence-preservation invariants (30), approval workflow with escalation (31), availability and degraded mode (32), pipeline loss versus backpressure (36), containment-claim verification (38), bounded fault injection (40), decision sensitivity (41) and a telemetry-limit experiment (42). Use synthetic, sanitized or explicitly authorized data only.

**Projects and assessment:** design studios on case intake and authorisation (Chapter 25) and coordinated recovery (Chapter 35); verification laboratories (30, 40); practicum scope and feedback (43); capstone design dossier (44), independent review (45), trial and recovery trial (46–47), evidence dossier (48), thesis (49) and defense (50). Every exercise carries a 0–3 rubric.

**Exercises and answers:** EX001–EX100, two per chapter, with worked results in the final appendix of the same book. The draft meets the publication-gate minimum of 50 exercises; the plan's target of three per chapter is not yet met.

**References and glossary:** verifiable primary sources and clear terms belong in the authored manuscript; linked project playbooks retain their source rights.
