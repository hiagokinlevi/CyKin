# Principal book plan — Network and communications security / Engineering course / Português

**One book for this course and language.** 50 curricular subjects are its chapters. Duration: 5 years; planned effort: approximately 4000 hours. Audience: Security systems designers. Entry knowledge: Calculus, computing, networks and systems engineering. Intended outcome: design, build and validate complex systems under constraints in network and communications security.

**Editorial state:** structured outline; manuscript not yet authored. English working summaries await translation and review.

## Book requirements

A finished book must contain at least 300 actual A4 pages and 50 substantive exercises with worked results. The current 1500-page budget is a plan, not measured pages. Every chapter needs teaching, definitions, a worked example, practical activity, errors and limits, exercises and a synthesis. The final appendix holds answers keyed to the exercise IDs. Cite verifiable sources, label synthetic cases, and review for technical accuracy, rights and accessibility.
