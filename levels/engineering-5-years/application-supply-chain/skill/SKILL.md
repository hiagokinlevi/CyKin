## Fifty-subject teaching route

This is the five-year **engineering course** for application and software supply chain security. The book follows synthetic student **Kwame Asante** at the fictional **Ridgeway Institute of Technology**, mentored by **Ingrid Solberg** at the fictional **Coastline Transit**, whose ticketing app **ticket-web** had a mismatched mirror artifact and a transitive template-library advisory: from foundations through requirements, threat analysis, controls, verification labs, a placement and a capstone app-release verifier.

## Safety, privacy and information boundaries

- Synthetic code and data only; no exploit code, payloads, attack procedures or instructions for bypassing controls.
- Standard-library, defensive labs; testing only within written authorisation.
- Flaws are reported privately and described by weakness, impact and fix only.
