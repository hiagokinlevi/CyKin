## Fifty-chapter engineering route

This is the five-year **engineering course** in information security. The book follows synthetic student **Lindiwe Dube**, supervised by **Prof. Marcus Hale** at the fictional **Brightwater Polytechnic**, securing the fictional **Saltmarsh Logistics**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | Mathematics, physics, programming, communication, statistics, architecture and ethics | EX001–EX030 |
| 11–20 | Networks, operating systems, data systems, software, security fundamentals, architecture, identity and standards context | EX031–EX060 |
| 21–30 | Requirements, asset models, threats, methods, design studio, architecture, controls, implementation, measurement and verification I | lab_assets (22), lab_baseline (28), lab_logins (29); EX061–EX090 |
| 31–40 | MFA rollout, reliability, resilience, integration, design studio II, scale, operations, validation, maintainability and verification II | lab_restore (32); EX091–EX120 |
| 41–50 | Optimisation, research, practice, capstone, review, recovery trial, dossier, thesis and defense | EX121–EX150 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): Asset register 2,400 vs scan 2,373: 90 registered not seen, 63 unregistered, 97.3% of scanned hosts registered; baseline on 300 servers: 92.7%, 84.3%, 92.3%, 216 (72.0%) pass all three; 1,672 failed logins over 863 accounts, 3 burst accounts flagged, slow client (61 failures) not flagged; restore of 500 files: 5 missing, 3 mismatched, 492 (98.4%) verified.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
