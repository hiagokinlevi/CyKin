## Fifty-chapter engineering route

This is the five-year **engineering course** in data governance. The book follows synthetic student **Kofi Mensah**, supervised by **Prof. Elena Varga** at the fictional **Northfield Polytechnic**, governing meter and sensor data at the fictional **Tidewater Water**.

| Chapters | Read and teach | Labs / exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | Mathematics, measurement, programming, communication, statistics, devices and ethics | EX001–EX030 |
| 11–20 | Networks, systems, data systems, security, systems engineering and governance context | EX031–EX060 |
| 21–30 | Requirements, models, threats, methods, design studio, controls, implementation, measurement and verification I | lab_lineage (22), lab_rules (28), lab_sla (29); EX061–EX090 |
| 31–40 | Catalogue, reliability, safety, integration, design studio II, scale, operations, validation, maintainability and verification II | lab_reconcile (32); EX091–EX120 |
| 41–50 | Optimisation, research, practice, capstone, review, recovery trial, dossier, thesis and defense | EX121–EX150 |

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): Lineage: 7 upstream nodes, 2 unowned (manual reads, raw flow); rules: 99.5%, 98.0%, 99.0%, 9,650 of 10,000 (96.5%) pass all; feed late on 11 of 90 days (12.2%), all 7 in week 40–46; reconciliation of 8,000 reads: 120 missing, 60 mismatched, 97.75% reconciled.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they score, simulate and check, and never probe real systems. Records and people are invented; no real organisational or personal data is processed.
- Cite laws by article and standards by edition; paraphrase rather than reproduce copyrighted standard text. Legal and standards analysis here is teaching, not legal or certification advice, and a synthetic audit is never presented as a real assessment.
