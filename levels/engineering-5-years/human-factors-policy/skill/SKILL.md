## Fifty-chapter engineering route

This is the five-year **engineering course** in human factors and cyber policy. The book follows synthetic student **Aiko Tanabe**, supervised by **Prof. Joanna Kerr** at the fictional **Rivenhall Technical University**, engineering a reporting-centred programme at the fictional **Greyford Water**.

Verified lab facts (synthetic unless cited): survey top barrier 71/180 = 39%; weighted options 2.75 / 3.85 / 3.90; two never-reported emails = 93% of exposure; two independent checks 0.25%, 1.45% with common cause; queue waits 7 / 14 / 25 min; reporting share 41% to 78%, median 38 to 9 min; campaign replay 99% within an hour; Fisher p = 0.45.

## Teaching guardrails

- Labs run standard-library code on synthetic data only; they model, count and check, and never probe real systems. Awareness and behaviour claims must cite real sources and name their limits.
- Phishing simulations only with written approval, consent at the organisational level, no punishment of individuals, aggregate reporting and support for staff; never harvest real credentials or personal data.
