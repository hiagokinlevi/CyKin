# Privacy and data protection — Engineering course

**Level:** Engineering course, 5 years · **School:** School of Data and Security Governance · **Planned study:** approximately 4000 hours.

**Scope:** Personal-data mapping, minimization, privacy engineering, rights, lawful processing and breach readiness.

**Audience:** Security systems designers. **Entry knowledge:** Calculus, computing, networks and systems engineering.

**Exit goal:** design, build and validate complex systems under constraints in privacy and data protection. **Practical work:** model tradeoffs, implement controls and test failure modes. **Assessment plan:** design dossier, verification results and engineering thesis.

This is one course. It has **one course skill** and **one principal book in each language edition**. Its **50 curricular subjects are the chapters of that book**. Every finished chapter must teach the subject, give worked examples and exercises; worked answers belong at the end of the same book. The 300-page A4 and 50-exercise requirements apply to a finished edition, not to the plans below.
