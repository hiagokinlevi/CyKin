## Fifty-chapter engineering route

This is the five-year **engineering degree** for identity and access management. The book follows synthetic student **Sana Malik** of the fictional **Brackenford Institute of Technology**, supervised by **Dr Owen Hale**, as she replaces the fictional **Linmouth Borough Council**'s fragmented local accounts with a central identity provider, single sign-on, phishing-resistant authentication, role-based access and HR-driven joiner, mover and leaver automation.
