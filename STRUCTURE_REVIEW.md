## Completion measure

The folder and plan scaffold covers **207 of 207 courses and 1,863 of 1,863 planned editions (100% of the current catalog's structural coverage)**. This is a coverage measure, not an educational-completion score. Every edition still requires authored teaching, chapter exercises and worked answers, source and technical review, and an A4 page check. **Completed editions: 0 of 1,863 (0%); 1,863 of 1,863 (100%) remain to pass the book gate.** Three English manuscripts are drafts and do not count as completed books.

At the chapter-planning level, **216 of 4,186 English briefs (5.16%)** have been individually tailored to their subject: all 182 Incident Response chapters and 34 Cybersecurity chapters. **3,970 (94.84%)** English briefs remain template-based and need editorial refinement before they can be treated as complete chapter designs. Non-English outlines are English working copies; no translated edition has been completed. A single percentage for all authoring work would be misleading until completed pages, exercises and reviews are measured consistently.

The 23-area curriculum consolidates the earlier, overly fragmented specialization scaffold. Its generated folders were removed from the public reading tree. The two previously authored manuscripts were integrated into the new Cybersecurity and Security Governance course folders; no authored chapter was discarded. The earlier catalog and playbook mapping are retained in the private construction workspace for migration traceability. Fourteen linked source playbooks were reassigned to the broader areas. The [public area map](AREA_MAP.md) distinguishes this project taxonomy from official frameworks and cites the ACM/IEEE/AIS/IFIP curricular guidance, NIST and ENISA.

## What a reader can use now

All nine [Incident Response course levels](levels/rapid-1-month/incident-response-resilience/README.md) now have distinct subject sequences, with shared foundations retained where appropriate. All **182 English chapter briefs** specify a distinct purpose, concepts, synthetic example, activity and learning sequence. The nine English plans also link [primary-source starting points](SOURCE_AUDIT.md#incident-response-primary-source-leads); full chapter bibliographies remain pending. The [rapid book draft](levels/rapid-1-month/incident-response-resilience/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) has initial teaching for all four subjects, 50 exercises and worked results. Its [subject index](levels/rapid-1-month/incident-response-resilience/en/SUBJECTS.md) links the course skill and marks all four chapters as drafts. The rapid lessons still need substantial expansion and technical review; the other eight books need authored chapters.

An internal structural pass found no duplicate chapter title within any Incident Response course, matched all 182 briefs to their grade and checked the planned study hours, pages and exercise allocations. Specialist review of the proposed depth and cases is still pending. The [curriculum grid](CURRICULUM_GRID.md) now shows exercise targets that rise with study level; these allocations are plans, not written exercises or completed-book evidence.

The [Cybersecurity rapid draft](levels/rapid-1-month/cybersecurity/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) contains three chapters and 15 worked exercises. Its four rapid chapter briefs now match the existing manuscript, and the 12 professional and 18 technical briefs have distinct subject-specific plans. The [Security Governance rapid draft](levels/rapid-1-month/security-governance-risk-compliance/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) contains one chapter and five worked exercises. These are available for study as drafts and will be edited in the English production cycle.

Each level links all 23 area course homes; each course home links its one skill, nine editions and separate playbooks. Every course has one book; its subjects are chapters with planned explanations, examples and exercises. Chapter summaries are **not** authored lessons. Non-English plans remain structural placeholders until translation, technical review and language editing.

## Publication and rights

Original maintainer-owned text is dedicated under [CC0 1.0](LICENSE), subject to [rights exceptions](RIGHTS.md). Linked third-party material and independent repositories retain their own rights. The [book standard](BOOK_STANDARD.md) requires an original complete manuscript, at least 50 substantial exercises with worked answers, technical and editorial review, accessible Markdown and at least 300 actual A4 pages in a fixed verification layout. No current draft passes that gate.
