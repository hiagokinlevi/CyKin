Each course's **draft coverage** is authored English subject-chapters ÷ planned chapters; a chapter counts after at least 300 words of teaching before its exercises. Manuscript words include exercises, answers and references. The **A4 preflight** column is a separate length check against the 300-page minimum, shown only when a private measured-page record matches the current manuscript's SHA-256. It becomes *Stale; rerender* as soon as the source changes. A page-floor percentage is **not** an educational-completion score: technical, source, rights, accessibility and editorial reviews still apply. Every course below has **0 verified complete English books**. The other eight language editions await their production turn. All values are generated locally before publication, not calculated live by GitHub.
