# CyberABC learning and editorial roadmap

**Authorship and editorial responsibility:** [hiagokinlevi](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi). This is the public learner-facing plan. Construction tools and migration records remain in the private CyberABC Engine.

## Current curriculum

The [academic map](AREA_MAP.md) has **23 broad areas in five schools**. Every [level](levels/README.md) contains all 23 area courses, each with nine language editions: **207 distinct course designs** and **1,863 planned language editions**. Each course has one principal book per language, with one curricular subject per chapter. English has **4,186 planned subjects** and **207 course skills**; 336 English briefs now have individual teaching outlines and 3,850 remain working summaries. The other language plans await translation. Three English manuscript drafts are public, and **no book is yet complete**. See the [progress review](STRUCTURE_REVIEW.md).

## English production, beginning with Incident Response

1. **Write and validate the books.** Finish the [rapid Incident Response book](levels/rapid-1-month/incident-response-resilience/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md): all four initial chapters and **50 exercises with corresponding worked results** are drafted. Chapter 1 has a readiness workshop. Chapter 2 has evidence-integrity and temporal-coverage labs plus optional delayed-arrival and evidence-package audit activities. Chapter 3 has guided booking reconciliation, a separate read-only CSV comparison and a cited real-incident source-reading activity. Chapter 4 has a six-artifact model tabletop, an objective-to-evidence measurement contract, a model after-action report and an optional approval-route replay lab. All **50 numbered exercises** specify objectives, inputs, outputs and assessment criteria; the chapter answers show evidence, limits and decisions. Deepen the teaching across the book, verify sources and accuracy, edit for accessibility, then render and inspect a release edition against the 300-page minimum. The current **36,652-word** draft produced **78 A4 pages** in a dated local preflight; see the [method and source hash](STRUCTURE_REVIEW.md#a4-preflight). This is still an incomplete book and the page count will change with edits.
2. Refine the English chapter plans alongside book writing. The tailored Incident Response set has 182 briefs; Cybersecurity has 154 of 182. Its remaining 28 advanced briefs and the other 3,822 English briefs are editorial work, but they do not substitute for authored lessons. Resolve the Incident Response specialist-review findings as the first book advances.
3. Continue the [Incident Response area](levels/rapid-1-month/incident-response-resilience/README.md) through professional, technical, technologist, bachelor's, engineering, postgraduate, master's and doctoral research levels. All nine levels now have subject-specific English grids; review their technical depth and dependencies while writing each distinct book.
4. Work through the remaining 22 areas in English, maintaining one distinct book per course. The earlier [Cybersecurity](levels/rapid-1-month/cybersecurity/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) and [Security Governance](levels/rapid-1-month/security-governance-risk-compliance/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) drafts remain available but do not change the new writing priority.
5. Review and edit the **entire English set** for technical accuracy, progression, source rights, exercises, accessibility and language. Render each book to A4 and verify the [completion gate](BOOK_STANDARD.md) before changing its status to complete.
