# CyberABC learning and editorial roadmap

**Authorship and editorial responsibility:** [hiagokinlevi](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi). This is the public learner-facing plan. Construction tools and migration records remain in the private CyberABC Engine.

## Current curriculum

The [academic map](AREA_MAP.md) has **23 broad areas in five schools**. Every [level](levels/README.md) contains all 23 area courses, each with nine language editions: **207 distinct course designs** and **1,863 planned language editions**. Each course has one principal book per language, with one curricular subject per chapter. English has **4,186 planned subjects** and **207 course skills**; 34 English chapter briefs have individual teaching outlines and the other 4,152 remain working summaries. The other language plans await translation. Three English manuscript drafts are public, and **no book is yet complete**. See the [progress review](STRUCTURE_REVIEW.md).

Expand each chapter with deeper explanations, fully specified exercises and reproducible labs; verify the sources and technical accuracy; edit for accessibility; then render and inspect the A4 edition against the 300-page minimum.

All nine levels now have subject-specific English grids; review their technical depth and dependencies while writing each distinct book.
4. Work through the remaining 22 areas in English, maintaining one distinct book per course. The earlier [Cybersecurity](levels/rapid-1-month/cybersecurity/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) and [Security Governance](levels/rapid-1-month/security-governance-risk-compliance/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) drafts remain available but do not change the new writing priority.
5. Review and edit the **entire English set** for technical accuracy, progression, source rights, exercises, accessibility and language. Render each book to A4 and verify the [completion gate](BOOK_STANDARD.md) before changing its status to complete.
