# Course duration and lesson quality

Each academic area offers one course at each of nine levels and one principal book per language edition. Curricular subjects are **modules**. A lesson is one chapter. On the weekday calendar, one lesson is studied on each of the five weekdays. The counts below are **planning minimums per course and theme**. They are not a maximum. If an idea needs more lessons to be taught well, the course grows. A chapter is as long as that idea needs. Do not pad a chapter, and do not add an empty lesson just to reach the minimum.

Author and deepen the **English** lesson first. Translate only after that English teaching exists. These rows are not claims that the editions are authored or accredited.

The minimums follow five weekday lessons and the level's duration: 20, 130, 390, 780, 1,040, 1,300, 520, 520 and 520. There is no upper cap. Page count is not a writing target. See [the book standard](BOOK_STANDARD.md).

The subject-module counts are the current index, not a ceiling. Keep authored text. Add a real English chapter when the next idea needs its own example and exercise. Do not mark an outline as a lesson. A translated plan is not a published lesson.
