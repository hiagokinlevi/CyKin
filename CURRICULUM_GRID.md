# Daily course cadence by level

Each academic area offers one course at each of nine levels and one principal book per language edition. Curricular subjects are **modules**. Every instructional weekday has **one book chapter/lesson**, with technical teaching, a worked example, a substantive exercise and a worked answer. The targets below apply to `en`, `pt`, `es`, `he`, `fr`, `ru`, `de`, `zh` and `ja`; they are not claims that those editions are already authored or accredited.

The normalized schedule uses four weeks for the rapid month, 26 for six months, 78 for 18 months and 52 per year for longer tracks. These are **instructional weeks**, independent of start date, holidays and vacations; actual study pace may vary. The A4 floor is `300 + max(0, weekday lessons − 20)`, so longer courses have larger minimum books. It is an editorial floor, never a substitute for technical depth or a verified PDF page count. See [the book standard](BOOK_STANDARD.md).

Existing plans and manuscripts use earlier subject-chapter counts (4, 12, 18, 30, 40, 50, 12, 8, 8). Those counts are **legacy module coverage**, not daily-lesson coverage. During migration, preserve authored content and show current chapter availability separately from the daily target. English production and review precede Portuguese, Spanish and the remaining editions. A translated plan is not a published lesson.
