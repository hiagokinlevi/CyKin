# Detailed OpenCyber Academy area map

Each area is taught at every formation level. The school is a grouping for navigation; the course folder sits under its level. Scope boundaries and prerequisites are editorial guides that require subject-expert review.

## Cybersecurity

- School: School of Cybersecurity Foundations.
- Scope: Foundations, threats, controls, defense lifecycle, evidence and authorized practice across digital systems.
- Main subareas: Threats and assets; defensive controls; response lifecycle; evidence basics.
- Foundational knowledge: Confidentiality, integrity, availability; system boundaries; authorized practice.
- Boundary: Introduces the whole field; detailed privacy, engineering and response methods belong to their dedicated areas.
- Recommended foundation: Basic digital literacy and safe practice.
- Competence: Explain a risk scenario, select a basic control and document what evidence would test it.
- Related areas: [Information security](levels/rapid-1-month/information-security/README.md), [Security architecture and engineering](levels/rapid-1-month/security-architecture/README.md), [Incident response and cyber resilience](levels/rapid-1-month/incident-response-resilience/README.md).
- Professional application: Security literacy, junior analysis and cross-team communication.
- Existing project to review: [hiagokinlevi/dfir-attack-lab](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi/dfir-attack-lab).
- Reuse decision: Review the dfir-attack-lab triage walkthrough as a candidate guided case; retain its source rights.
- Supplementary playbooks: [source index](playbooks/cybersecurity/README.md).
- Area-specific gaps: Write sourced introductory cases and contrast preventive, detective and recovery controls.
- Cross-cutting gaps: full books, source review, accessible labs, translations and measured A4 editions.
- Courses: [Rapid course](levels/rapid-1-month/cybersecurity/README.md), [Professional course](levels/professional-6-months/cybersecurity/README.md), [Technical course](levels/technical-18-months/cybersecurity/README.md), [Technologist course](levels/technologist-3-years/cybersecurity/README.md), [Bachelor's course](levels/bachelors-4-years/cybersecurity/README.md), [Engineering course](levels/engineering-5-years/cybersecurity/README.md), [Postgraduate course](levels/postgraduate-2-years/cybersecurity/README.md), [Master's course](levels/masters-2-years/cybersecurity/README.md), [Doctoral research track](levels/doctorate-2-years/cybersecurity/README.md).

## Information security

- School: School of Cybersecurity Foundations.
- Scope: Protect information confidentiality, integrity and availability through policy, classification, controls and assurance.
- Main subareas: Information classification; access rules; data loss prevention; assurance.
